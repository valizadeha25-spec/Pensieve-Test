# Business Model

The Product Domain covers product philosophy, feature rationale, and scale/growth thinking under Negin’s ownership, using CEO and product lead as internal operating titles.^\[1\]^ (`data:184`) PamAI’s commercial model remains a working product-business hypothesis: it is an important, live document rather than settled doctrine and is under active review by Negin.^\[2\]^ (`data:63`)

## Create value

PamAI names its core value “cognitive coherence”: removing the gap between knowing what matters and actually working on it continuously, with relief from holding the entire project in mind every day.^\[3\]^ (`data:63`) The value is designed to compound from cognitive coherence now into “compounding intelligence” with use: the longer Pam is used, the more it knows how a person thinks, decides, and drifts.^\[4\]^ (`data:63`) “Team coherence” is a stated future expansion of value and is not built.^\[5\]^ (`data:63`) The working moat is described as methodology plus compounding memory, not the underlying model.^\[6\]^ (`data:63`)

## Deliver value

The proposed delivery journey moves from project definition into an ongoing daily rhythm:

1. **Define** — a conversational Foundation session extracts seven fields and builds two outputs: a plan for the user and a mind for Pam.^\[7\]^ (`data:63`)
2. **Confirm** — the user reads the plan, pushes back if it does not resonate, and Pam edits it.^\[8\]^ (`data:63`)
3. **Reality setup** — Pam connects the calendar and allocates the project into the user’s actual week.^\[9\]^ (`data:63`)
4. **Daily rhythm** — a morning brief selects three tasks from the plan and calendar, chosen by Pam rather than the user.^\[10\]^ (`data:63`)

The delivery extensions include a downloadable and shareable documentation home described as “Pam’s memory, and also yours. No scattered Google Docs.”^\[11\]^ (`data:63`) Collaboration currently means two individual Pam users sharing one project, explicitly not team management.^\[12\]^ (`data:63`) The delivery moat is that Pam becomes the only place the project lives—thinking, tracking, documents, and sharing in one continuous environment—rather than merely a chat interface or morning brief.^\[13\]^ (`data:63`) The proposed channel is founder-to-founder and community-seeded: the value is hard to advertise, easy to feel once described, and spreads through testimony.^\[14\]^ (`data:63`)

## Capture value

The model retains a mechanism-level hypothesis that capture should follow what Pam actually does rather than seats; it also names a tension between a visible usage meter and the promise to “show up daily and trust the rhythm.”^\[15\]^ (`data:63`)

Pricing remains unresolved. The current product-philosophy index marks credit pricing as stale and removed entirely and says Amir is building a different pricing/capture model; no live price is stated here.^\[16\]^ (`data:189`)

The working model leaves team and multi-seat monetization open, noting that the credit-era version called collaboration “the on-ramp” and team capture “the unanswered half.”^\[17\]^ (`data:63`)
