# Business & Strategy

PamAI’s founding direction is a partner that takes responsibility for the plan rather than another tool for users to operate.^\[1\]^[^data:35] Its commercial model is explicitly unfinished and under active review by Negin.^\[2\]^[^data:189] This page holds durable strategic choices, value logic, and unresolved trade-offs; detailed product mechanics, market execution, and finance stay in dedicated pages.

## Strategic thesis

Pam began from Amir’s own failure mode: going deep into learning and building could make him lose sight of the bigger picture.^\[3\]^[^data:35] The team chose first-principles redesign over copying task boards, Gantt charts, and calendar conventions, asking what a project actually is.^\[4\]^[^data:35]

Negin’s recorded product rationale makes the main strategic trade-off explicit: Pam was not intended as a point solution improving one step of an existing workflow, but as a redesign of how a project gets done as a whole; she therefore argued that the usual “simple MVP, one task” maturity bar did not fully apply.^\[5\]^[^data:41]

## Value creation and delivery

PamAI names the value it creates “cognitive coherence”: removing the gap between knowing what matters and actually working on it continuously, with relief from carrying the project in mind every day.^\[6\]^[^data:63] The value is intended to compound into intelligence about how a person thinks, decides, and drifts, while “team coherence” remains a future direction rather than a built capability.^\[7\]^[^data:63]

The delivery thesis is an integrated project home: thinking, tracking, documents, and sharing in one continuous environment, rather than a chat interface or isolated morning brief.^\[8\]^[^data:63] A related operating discipline is to build each road only after its friction has been felt, because every system adds maintenance, cleanup, and mental or token cost.^\[9\]^[^data:35]

## Value capture and open decisions

Pricing is not settled. The old credit tiers in `business-model.md` were withdrawn and must not be cited; Amir is still developing the final capture model.^\[10\]^[^data:189] The shipped product nevertheless had live credit-based billing on 27 August 2026: the observed Free account showed a non-expiring credit wallet, monthly-credit subscription and top-ups.^\[11\]^[^data:273] Team and multi-seat monetization remain unresolved; the model describes collaboration as the on-ramp and team capture as the unanswered half.^\[12\]^[^data:63]

Future directions should remain visibly separate from commitments. The idea bank is explicitly “not a roadmap, not commitments”;^\[13\]^[^data:58] one adjacent option under active evaluation is an R&D tax-credit and innovation-documentation wedge, but it notes an existing UK competitor and says the project-intent differentiation is unconfirmed.^\[14\]^[^data:58]

## Entity and legal structure

PamAI is not yet incorporated: the privacy policy names Amirmohammad Valizadehmehrabani, "an individual (not yet incorporated)", as the controller of the personal data PamAI processes.^\[15\]^[^data:9]^\[16\]^[^data:9]

Whether to incorporate, when, and in what shape is a major open trade-off. A working exploration filed on 2026-08-28 as input for Amir — the analysis is ChatGPT's read of Home Office guidance, not legal advice, and still needs validation from an immigration solicitor and company formation advisor — started from Negin asking ChatGPT whether registering PAM as a UK company now would affect her Innovator Founder visa eligibility (targeting a 2027 endorsement application).^\[17\]^[^data:265]^\[18\]^[^data:265]^\[19\]^[^data:265]^\[20\]^[^data:265]^\[21\]^[^data:266] Its conclusion: incorporating now does not hurt eligibility, provided PAM stays the demonstrably core, scalable venture, with the recommended framing "PAM Ltd = PAM".^\[22\]^[^data:265]^\[23\]^[^data:265]^\[24\]^[^data:266]

The structures worked through are a generic AI agency with PAM as a side product; a single company, PAM Technologies Ltd, designed as an AI product/technology company that also provides consulting services; and two separate entities, with a separate agency company as the potential home for a side consultancy run for cash flow.^\[25\]^[^data:266]^\[26\]^[^data:266]^\[27\]^[^data:266]^\[28\]^[^data:266] The analysis calls the single-company option the sweet spot for the founders' current situation, with PAM as the company's scalable core and consulting as supporting revenue, customer discovery and implementation.^\[29\]^[^data:266]^\[30\]^[^data:266]^\[31\]^[^data:266] The known cost: it is all one company, so investors buying shares effectively invest in the whole company, not merely the PAM product, and the risk is being potentially less attractive to some VCs if the company becomes too consultancy-heavy.^\[32\]^[^data:266]^\[33\]^[^data:266]^\[34\]^[^data:266]
