# Business & Strategy

PamAI’s founding direction is a partner that takes responsibility for the plan rather than another tool for users to operate.^\[1\]^ (`data:35`) Its commercial model is explicitly unfinished and under active review by Negin.^\[2\]^ (`data:189`) This page holds durable strategic choices, value logic, and unresolved trade-offs; detailed product mechanics, market execution, and finance stay in dedicated pages.

## Strategic thesis

Pam began from Amir’s own failure mode: going deep into learning and building could make him lose sight of the bigger picture.^\[3\]^ (`data:35`) The team chose first-principles redesign over copying task boards, Gantt charts, and calendar conventions, asking what a project actually is.^\[4\]^ (`data:35`)

Negin’s recorded product rationale makes the main strategic trade-off explicit: Pam was not intended as a point solution improving one step of an existing workflow, but as a redesign of how a project gets done as a whole; she therefore argued that the usual “simple MVP, one task” maturity bar did not fully apply.^\[5\]^ (`data:41`)

## Value creation and delivery

PamAI names the value it creates “cognitive coherence”: removing the gap between knowing what matters and actually working on it continuously, with relief from carrying the project in mind every day.^\[6\]^ (`data:63`) The value is intended to compound into intelligence about how a person thinks, decides, and drifts, while “team coherence” remains a future direction rather than a built capability.^\[7\]^ (`data:63`)

The delivery thesis is an integrated project home: thinking, tracking, documents, and sharing in one continuous environment, rather than a chat interface or isolated morning brief.^\[8\]^ (`data:63`) A related operating discipline is to build each road only after its friction has been felt, because every system adds maintenance, cleanup, and mental or token cost.^\[9\]^ (`data:35`)

## Value capture and open decisions

Pricing is not settled: the housekeeping record marks the earlier credit pricing stale and removed,^\[10\]^ (`data:189`) while the later 2026-08-15 entry records £36 per month for 1,000 credits as initial-testing pricing rather than the finalized structure.^\[11\]^ (`data:63`) Team and multi-seat monetization remain unresolved; the model describes collaboration as the on-ramp and team capture as the unanswered half.^\[12\]^ (`data:63`)

Future directions should remain visibly separate from commitments. The idea bank is explicitly “not a roadmap, not commitments”;^\[13\]^ (`data:58`) one adjacent option under active evaluation is an R&D tax-credit and innovation-documentation wedge, but it notes an existing UK competitor and says the project-intent differentiation is unconfirmed.^\[14\]^ (`data:58`)
