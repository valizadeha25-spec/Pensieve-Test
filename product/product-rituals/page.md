# Product Rituals

Pam's ritual layer organizes project work around six responsibilities: Foundation protects why, Planning protects what, Morning Brief protects today, Fulfillment protects what happened, Health protects truth over time, and Reflection protects conscious change.^\[1\]^ (`data:62`)

Pam carries the project complexity while the user does the doing. The user does three tasks a day and informally updates Pam at the end of the day; storing, documenting, planning, deciding, and reflecting sit with Pam.^\[2\]^ (`data:65`) Amir's build-in-public draft frames the user's essential orientation as two answers: what to do today and where the user is in the roadmap.^\[3\]^ (`data:37`)

## Intended cadence

A business-model working draft, explicitly marked unfinished and under active review, describes four stages before the daily rhythm begins.^\[4\]^ (`data:63`)

1. **Define** — a conversational Foundation session builds two outputs: a plan for the user and a mind for Pam.^\[5\]^ (`data:63`)
2. **Confirm** — the user reads the plan, pushes back if it does not resonate, and Pam edits it.^\[6\]^ (`data:63`)
3. **Reality Setup** — Pam connects the calendar and allocates the project into the user's actual week.^\[7\]^ (`data:63`)
4. **Daily rhythm** — the morning brief contains three tasks from the plan and calendar, chosen by Pam rather than the user.^\[8\]^ (`data:63`)

Amir's build-in-public draft gives the repeating loop more texture: Pam's morning brief has one top task and two supporting tasks chosen by Pam; it closes the day by recording what moved, what did not, and what was learned; it watches the project silently between sessions; and it hosts a fortnightly reflection.^\[9\]^ (`data:37`)

## What is settled

The product-rituals note names Morning Brief, Fulfillment, Project Health, Reflection, and Reality Setup, but says those skills must be learned and enabled through direct conversation with Negin rather than reconstructed from old source documents.^\[10\]^ (`data:69`) The operating rituals are marked as cleared pending a fresh walkthrough, and the guidance is not to reconstruct the old text.^\[11\]^ (`data:70`)

This page therefore records the durable ritual family and its working cadence, not a definitive skill-by-skill specification. The broader product surface is covered on [Product](../page.md), while the process and signals used to evaluate these rituals belong on [Product Testing](../product-testing/page.md).
