# Content Library

Content Library is the persistent home for outside social inspiration that can be analyzed, indexed, tagged, and retrieved for later content ideation.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/31) It is intended to be a browsable, persistent, tagged corpus that agents can search and synthesise against, rather than a report generated and then lost.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45)

## Lifecycle

1. **Capture.** An iOS shortcut sends an Instagram URL to a Supabase queue with one tap.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35)
2. **Analyze and ingest.** A Codex routine wakes every 6 hours, downloads the media, reads it frame by frame, transcribes the audio, analyzes it deeply, ingests it to the wiki, indexes and tags it for search, and files it in the library.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35)
3. **Retrieve and ideate.** Amir opens the library, picks items onto a board, and brainstorms against a format already proven on social.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35)

## Indexing and retrieval

The content taxonomy distinguishes three layers: the platform vessel, the content style or format, and the internal structure used to pace retention.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231) A single piece can combine all three—for example, a reel shot as greenscreen using an expert-debunk structure.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231)

The format taxonomy offers a reference catalog of about 28 named styles, but it is an open-ended starting vocabulary: new formats should be added as they are observed rather than spun into new pages.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231) It also proposes five format-selection metadata axes—effort, frequency, speed of result, planning, and niche fit—rather than treating those properties as formats themselves.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231) The mapping of those terms to the internal vocabulary and whether the tradeoff axes should become structured metadata in a real library store remain open questions.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231)

The catalog is a candidate seed for the content-library format taxonomy in design, and it is not a fit for the existing `format` field: the catalog's format means content style, while that field is currently scoped to vessel only.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231) [^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231) A further candidate term, "default format", proposes another low-friction category: film visible work that already repeats, then preserve a researched hook and reusable script/format skeleton across variations.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231) "Default format" is a system property, not one fixed visual style—order fulfillment, cleaning, organizing, art production, real estate, or day-in-the-life can all qualify when the underlying activity naturally recurs—and it remains a candidate term, not controlled performance proof.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231) [^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231)

The Instagram craft references map hooks, story-flow structures, retention mechanics, and visuals to `hook_structure`, `storyline`, `retention_devices`, and `visual_type`. Ingested reels are tagged on those axes, and `board-ideation` surfaces the resulting moves as a parts-bin ranked by proven reach.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/27)

## Operating principle

The system began as an empty directory; each step was added on demand as friction appeared rather than designed up front.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/31) The resulting infrastructure evolved and adapted to Amir’s workflow, holding in the environment thinking that otherwise had to be repeated.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35) The operating discipline is to build each road only after feeling the friction by hand, because growth is not free: every system needs maintenance, cleanup, and mental and token cost.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35)

## Relationship to channel research

The Content Library requirement arose in LinkedIn research because `linkedin-scout` was assessed as “really badly configured, not that much helpful”; the requested replacement was the persistent corpus described above, not a disposable report.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45) As of 2026-08-25, the LinkedIn execution cycle is deliberately parked pending a separate research round.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45)

The scout practice itself kept running while the cycle sat parked: the 2026-08-31 run was the first routine scout after a 2.5-month gap, covering five clusters — AI-hype reality-check, solo-founder, build-in-public, non-technical founder, and honest-signal / practitioner takes — and filing its pattern findings as a dated wiki synthesis that cross-links the captured examples it draws on.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271) Its headline patterns — contradiction as the dominant hook formula across all clusters, and text-only dominance — are part of the platform-craft picture maintained on [LinkedIn](../../../content-brand/channels/linkedin/page.md).[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271)[^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271) The run also measured the capture pipeline itself: harvestapi returned 44–50 posts per run at a 4/5 rating, while scarletapi returned zero posts across all five clusters, a systemic failure with a removal proposal filed in the main session.[^\[25\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271)[^\[26\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271)[^\[27\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271) Whether that removal was actioned remained an open question for the next scout run.[^\[28\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271)
