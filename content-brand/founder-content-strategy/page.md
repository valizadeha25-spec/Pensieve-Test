# Founder Content Strategy

Amir’s founder content is the personal lane for communicating the work of building AI systems: personal brand, thought leadership, and build-in-public documentation, intentionally rawer and more in-the-moment than Pam’s company account.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) The company account handles trend-riding and brand voice; this lane handles the founding story, teaching, and personal reality.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

## Position and audience

Amir’s niche is AI practitioner / founder building in public, focused on the problems that emerge when people actually build and operate AI systems rather than theorise about them.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/92) The confirmed audience has two non-engineer groups: ambitious people without a traditional CS background who want to build with AI, and business owners, freelancers, managers, and employees who want AI productivity but feel locked out.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) The account is not for engineers.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Amir’s [confirmed niche decision](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/222) fixes this boundary.

The core tension is management burden rather than tool novelty: “I was the AI’s manager, and it was exhausting.”[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/92) Pam’s why frames it as knowing which action has the most leverage while carrying both worker and manager roles.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/21) Amir built Pam for his own failure mode; going deep fast into learning, building, and problem-solving made it hard to step back and see the whole picture.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35) August 2026 scouting frames AI value around human judgment, domain knowledge, and curiosity rather than tool access.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271) It also found most solo-founder content analysing the phenomenon from outside, while practitioners writing from inside their own live build are rare—a positioning gap Amir can occupy.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227)

Positioning begins with a concrete viewer frustration, assumption, or desired change, presented as a diagnosis or mechanism rather than a broad category or biography.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/316) For a narrow founder audience, an explicit filter can name the recurring “will Claude or ChatGPT replicate this?” fear, accept it instead of denying it, and turn it into a choice about an industry’s specific workflows. The vertical-workflow claim is a hypothesis, not proof that competition cannot replicate it.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/314) [^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/315) [^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/316)

## Editorial portfolio

The portfolio has four pillars: building in public, education on AI engineering, honest signal on AI, and personal story; their weights are not set.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) It should balance education, storytelling, and authority/results proof—education teaches, storytelling creates identification, and authority proof establishes trust.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/235)

Owned topics are drift and lost context, the demo-to-production gap, custom tooling, and the build-in-public founder arc.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/92) August scouting found top build-in-public examples centered on identity and transformation, while generic progress updates did not appear in the top tier.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271) The custom-tooling thesis is “Software should adapt to you, not you to the software.”[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/105) The broader agent conversation has moved from “Why do demos break in production?” to “We’ve deployed agents — how do we govern them?”—monitoring, rollback, policy enforcement, and a trust layer—but governance remains a market angle rather than an Amir-specific claim without a documented deployment incident.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271) [^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/97)

## Source material, voice, and packaging

The journey directory is first-party source material, not finished posts; it feeds Instagram build-in-public, LinkedIn founder posts, and Twitter threads.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/29) Its log is dated and tagged with moments, lessons, failures, decisions, and milestones, to be shaped into content later, while founding-story.md supplies the raw material for a four-to-six-post origin arc.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35) [^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/37)

Founder credibility comes from what was built, shipped, learned, and changed rather than broad self-description; when results are unknown, a bounded public experiment can create a credible position before a success story.[^\[25\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/316) Amir supplies the substance and voice: he writes, and support helps him write better rather than writing a post from scratch.[^\[26\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) Communication leads with the world, feeling, and wound before naming the solution, and build-in-public work leads with the problem and lived experience rather than the product, demo, or sales pitch.[^\[27\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/21) [^\[28\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Personal experience, unique perspective, and contrarian takes matter more than information alone.[^\[29\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/91)

Choose the format before the topic, then make every shoot-ready idea explicit in six fields: format, main insight, hook, storyline, retention, and visual plan.[six-field content brief](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/210)[^\[30\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) [^\[31\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) For video and written content, draw the emotional arc first—tension rises, releases briefly, rises again, then an earned payoff—before choosing the storyline; write custom lines from Amir’s supplied facts, experience, and opinion.[^\[32\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/313) Reusable structure and production mechanics are on [Production Craft](../channels/instagram/production-craft/page.md) and [Content Development](../content-development/page.md).

## Channels and operating plan

Amir’s member profile still says “Primary channel: LinkedIn,” but the active [platform decision](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/209) makes Instagram primary, LinkedIn second, and Twitter/X and YouTube Shorts parked; Instagram’s opening vessel is reels only.[^\[33\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) [^\[34\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46)

Instagram targets five reels a week on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Sunday, using talking-head and build-in-public as the backbone, rotating reach experiments, and a very-low-effort anti-miss tier.[^\[35\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) The operating plan separates a strategic floor of three Instagram reels from a target of five, sets LinkedIn at a two-post floor and four-post target, and includes one lead magnet a week; its 90-day outcome targets are 10,000 average reel views and 5,000 LinkedIn followers.[^\[36\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46)

LinkedIn remains a two-post weekly floor and four-post target, but its execution cycle is deliberately parked pending a research round under [the LinkedIn execution decision](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/226).[^\[37\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/38) Current scouting favors 200–500-word posts and a Problem → Insight → Implication arc; contradiction is the dominant observed hook. These observations guide experiments, not claims about Amir’s own performance.[^\[38\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227)

X is parked because daily posting plus 2–3 replies was not credible on top of the Instagram and LinkedIn commitments, and its cadence is not counted in the volume budget.[X parking decision](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/225)[^\[39\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/47) Detailed channel execution is on [Instagram](../channels/instagram/page.md), [LinkedIn](../channels/linkedin/page.md), and [Twitter](../channels/twitter/page.md); cross-channel operating choices are on [Content Strategy](../content-strategy/page.md).

## Validation

The niche remains a hypothesis: no posts have yet been published and measured.[^\[40\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/92) Validation should optimise for audience quality—not follower count alone—using relevant replies, recognised expertise, qualified conversations, or durable association with the point of view.[^\[41\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/316) LinkedIn scouting supplies test inputs such as counter-intuitive reframes and first-person narratives paired with concrete outcomes, not proof that a sub-niche will resonate.[^\[42\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/139)

Still open are the pillar weights, the LinkedIn execution cycle, which sub-niche resonates, what Amir-specific evidence could support governance, and whether he has a deployed MCP integration to describe; the MCP signal is that “everyone ships MCP servers and almost no one describes them.”[^\[43\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) [^\[44\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/38) [^\[45\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/92) [^\[46\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/97) [^\[47\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271)
