# Original Content Frameworks

Original Content Frameworks is Amir’s incubation space for ideas that are too large for one piece of content. Concepts are developed across sessions until they are argued, mechanized, bounded, and named; a concept file is the source from which content compiles, not the deliverable itself.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32)

The working boundary is explicit: `log.md` holds dated raw lived moments, `teaching-bank.md` holds concepts Amir already understands and is ready to teach, and `concepts/` holds frameworks still being figured out.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32)

## The concept record

Each concept file answers eight questions: thesis, concrete problem, prior art and delta, mechanism, evidence, where it does not work, open questions, and name plus one-liner.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32) The evidence slot requires Amir’s own builds with receipts, not vibes. The prior-art/delta and failure-boundary slots are the two gates: an ownable framework must say how it differs from what already exists and where it breaks.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32)

Those gates must come from Amir’s articulation and scars, surfaced through strategic questions, rather than from invented related work or failure modes.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32) The name arrives last and is derived from the delta, mechanism, and boundary rather than brainstormed in isolation.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32)

## Status ladder

The status ladder replaces a vague `developing` or `ready` label with the next missing move.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32) `seed` means a captured method or belief with no delta or boundary; `argued` means prior art and difference are resolved; `mechanized` means the model has named parts and receipts; `bounded` states where it fails; and `named` means it is teachable for months and can graduate to the teaching bank or anchor a content pillar.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32) Each file’s frontmatter carries `status`, `owner`, `started`, and a one-line `thesis` once the thesis slot exists.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32)

## Development discipline

Keep one file per concept and let it grow across sessions. Develop it when it blocks a shoot or when Amir wants to think, then compile it into posts rather than polishing a file nobody reads.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32) The process also watches for a shared spine: when multiple concept files start to rhyme, test whether they are facets of one underlying framework before developing both halves separately.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32)

The central quality rule is not to fabricate missing gates. An empty prior-art comparison or failure boundary is a useful status signal; filling either with an assistant’s inference would make the concept appear more defensible than Amir’s own evidence allows.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32)

## Graduation into teaching

A `named` concept is the graduation threshold: it should be teachable for months before it becomes a teaching-bank thread or anchors a content pillar.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/32) The teaching bank is first-party source material for educational and concept content, aimed at high-level explanations of what AI can do on the business side rather than Pam’s internals or deep technical implementation.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/42) Its audience is normal business people, not a technical crowd, so technical raw material must be re-expressed in plain language before delivery.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/42)

The teaching bank is raw input, not a ranked list of settled beliefs. Material captured when it was top-of-mind carries recency bias and must be synthesized over time before it is treated as a durable conviction.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/42) See [Agentic System Design](../../../ai-practice/agentic-system-design/page.md) for a detailed current concept treatment.
