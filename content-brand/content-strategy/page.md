# Content Strategy

PamAI's communications start from the human problem rather than a feature list.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/21) Publishing is channel-first: each platform has its own accounts, playbook, and working space, while shared doctrine and cross-channel material stay at the root.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/22)

## Strategic premise

PamAI's core narrative is about direction: the hard part of building is knowing which action brings the most leverage, not a lack of effort or intelligence.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/21) Every message should lead with the audience's changing world, existing feeling, and wound, then name what addresses it.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/21)

Market-positioning content makes fragmentation across tools, drift, and the need to re-explain context legible rather than turning the feed into a competitor comparison.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/20) Public posts should describe the pattern, not name competitors or products directly.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/20) Amir's build-in-public content may name Pam, but it still leads with the problem and lived experience, not a demo or sales pitch.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

## Account roles and recurring pillars

The portfolio gives each account a distinct job instead of treating every channel as one repurposed feed.

- **PAM on LinkedIn** uses The Problem to make founder drift, fog, and split legible without product mention.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/44) The POV takes a position on tools, work, AI, and management.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/44) The Proof shows concrete product moments, the daily cycle, and real use cases to create waitlist interest.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/44)
- **Amir's personal lane** is rawer, personal, and build-in-public oriented. Its defined audiences are ambitious people without a traditional CS background who want to build with AI, and business owners, freelancers, managers, and employees who want AI productivity; it does not target engineers.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Its four pillars are building in public, education on AI engineering, honest signal on AI, and personal story.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/51) The guardrail is receipts: state the specific thing, what it took, and what did not work, rather than making general AI promises.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)
- **Pam on Instagram** is the brand-reach channel, participating in platform culture through trends, humour, visual storytelling, and relatable moments rather than acting as a founder account.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/26) Its pillars are trend-riding, founder reality from Pam's voice, and natural product teases.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/26) Its voice is dry, funny, and self-aware: relatable first, brand second, and distinct from Amir's personal account.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/26)
- **Negin on LinkedIn** is a separate professional lane about product design judgment, defensible product positions, and decision rationale; Pam is the evidence rather than the plot.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/40) Her delivery is essayistic and argument-led, not a hook/listicle formula.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/40)
- **Teaching content** explains high-level, business-side AI concepts to normal business people, translating technical ideas into plain language rather than teaching Pam's internals.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/42)

Recurring themes can graduate from research when repetition supports a durable editorial lane. AI Hype vs. Reality is treated as a distinct theme: four sightings across two scout runs put it well past the rule-of-three threshold, and generic AI-is-overhyped framing was absent from the dataset — the theme requires a credibility anchor to work.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/228) The August 2026 scout round also fixed which rhetorical mechanisms are open to PamAI's own voices. The responsibility-shift moves the locus of threat from the AI tool to the person's own behavior, and contradiction/reframe names the hype frame and flips it in one sentence; these are now the two highest-performing mechanisms accessible to a mid-authority voice without borrowing external statistics or quoted reversals.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/228) A one-sentence reframe is a personal point-of-view assertion rather than a credibility-anchored claim, so it needs enough standing that the bald assertion reads as insight rather than opinion.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/228) The credibility-flip, by contrast, has matured: quoting a reversed authority without real standing now reads as borrowed credibility, not original point of view.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/228) The responsibility-shift carries a victim-blaming risk when framed too sharply; the question form seems the safer framing.[^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/228) Amir-specific content angles for this theme live in [Founder Content Strategy](../founder-content-strategy/page.md).

## Channel architecture and cadence

The doctrine has two clocks. Niche material answers who and what; strategy answers how much, when, and in what form; channel files adapt both for one platform, while playbooks hold craft.[^\[25\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/22)

The current platform priority is Instagram first, LinkedIn second, with Twitter/X and YouTube Shorts parked in the current operating plan.[^\[26\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) For Amir's personal channels, the plan sets an Instagram floor of 3 reels a week and a target of 5, a LinkedIn floor of 2 posts and a target of 4, and one lead magnet each week.[^\[27\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) Instagram's five reel days are Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Sunday.[^\[28\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Monday is the full edit, ideation, and lead-magnet day; Thursday and Saturday are shoot days, and the one-week rolling lag keeps finished inventory ahead of publication.[^\[29\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46)

The PAM company LinkedIn page runs 1–3 posts per week, rotating through The Problem, The POV, and The Proof; personal accounts sit on top of that company cadence.[^\[30\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/44) The PAM Instagram playbook sets 2–3 weekday pieces and coordinates its batch production with Amir's personal account on the same shoot days, using different content.[^\[31\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/26)

LinkedIn remains a deliberate exception: its execution cycle is parked as of 2026-08-25 pending platform research, even though its volume, separate-from-Instagram approach, shared weekly lead magnet, and independent streak are decided.[^\[32\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45) Post days, writing time, source material, and the mechanics that actually work on the platform remain open.[^\[33\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45)

## Workflow and governance

The publishing pipeline treats a post as one state-and-content row, keeps `posts.content` and `posts.content_brief` current, and binds Instagram production files back to the row; ideas come from sessions, the journey material well, or promoted wiki research.[^\[34\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/22)

Niyayesh owns content creation and execution across the platforms.[^\[35\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/19) Once a piece has its main insight, she synthesizes the weekly list from research, journey material, and brainstorming into 10 ideas and 5 chosen pieces, then owns format selection, visual planning, scheduling, and execution follow-through.[^\[36\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/19) A shoot-ready idea has six required fields: format, main insight, hook, storyline, retention, and visual plan.[^\[37\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

Every PAM and personal LinkedIn post receives one review before publishing—not as a gate, but as a second pair of eyes for voice consistency.[^\[38\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/44) Each piece also gets a job—discovery, authority, relationship, or conversion—so its opening, proof, next step, and review metric are chosen together.[^\[39\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/252) Beyond a post's own job, distribution is also judged by whether the right external surface can attribute Pam—a referral path, a community path, search or AI discoverability, or social credibility ahead of an application conversation.[^\[40\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/252) Distribution can also route attention into real-world conversation ahead of any owned audience or funnel: a physical sign points to a simple webpage, and the webpage routes back into an in-person conversation.[^\[41\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/252) The operating loop is to define the audience problem and position, choose a reusable format and job, build the opening and proof, publish into a route, and review the result against its job.[^\[42\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/233) Review separates attention, trust, conversion, and format failures, changing one major variable at a time rather than turning a single result into a permanent rule.[^\[43\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/233)

AI can compress research, production, repurposing, and reporting only after the strategic choices are made.[^\[44\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/233) In consequential workflows, AI handles aggregation and pattern detection while humans retain approval.[^\[45\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/296)

Platform research is directional, not timeless doctrine. A June 17 LinkedIn scout run observed stronger performance for founder voice than advisor or consultant voice and near-zero engagement for short teaser-with-link posts; these observations should inform tests without replacing the pending research round.[^\[46\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/141)

## Editorial voice and quality bar

PAM's LinkedIn voice is short, one idea per line, specific, concrete, calm, and direct; it avoids startup-ese, productivity rhetoric, emoji as punctuation, and selling.[^\[47\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/44) Amir's audience doctrine similarly prioritises specific honest takes, real examples, what does not work, and operational intelligence over generic advice.[^\[48\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/51) The teaching bank is raw input rather than a ranked list of beliefs: recent captures carry recency bias and should be synthesized over time.[^\[49\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/42)

The shortest durable rule is why before what, evidence before hype, and a distinct voice for each account.
