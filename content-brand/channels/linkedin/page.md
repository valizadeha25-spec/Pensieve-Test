# LinkedIn

Pam's LinkedIn presence is a shared working space for four accounts — the Pam company account plus personal accounts for Amir, Niyayesh, and Negin — with per-account playbooks, one database-backed post pipeline, and a research practice feeding the channel.^\[1\]^[^data:39] The most recent scout synthesis, dated 2026-08-31, adds the channel's freshest platform read: contradiction was the dominant hook formula across all five August clusters, and text-only posts dominated the top tier.^\[2\]^[^data:271]^\[3\]^[^data:271] The execution cycle is deliberately parked as of 2026-08-25: Amir decided to park it[^decision:226] until a LinkedIn-specific research round runs, because building one now would mean guessing at the mechanics of a platform that has not been studied.^\[4\]^[^data:45] Whatever gets built also serves Negin, so this is not only Amir's channel.^\[5\]^[^data:45]

For the cross-channel portfolio see [Channels](../page.md); Amir's niche and pillars are maintained on [Founder Content Strategy](../../founder-content-strategy/page.md), shared short-form craft on [Production Craft](../instagram/production-craft/page.md), and the research-corpus architecture on [Content Library](../../../systems/company-brain/content-library/page.md).

## Execution status

The strategy file fixes the channel's decided shape: priority second behind Instagram — whose cycle is designed while LinkedIn's is not —^\[6\]^[^data:46] volume at a 2 a week floor and 4 a week target,^\[7\]^[^data:45] posts written separate rather than repurposed from Instagram,^\[8\]^[^data:45] one lead magnet a week made Mondays and shared with Instagram,^\[9\]^[^data:45] and a LinkedIn streak carried separately from Instagram's.^\[10\]^[^data:45]

What is open: post days are unset — the streak cannot be built without them;^\[11\]^[^data:45] the writing block is unresolved between a daily habit block, a fixed weekly block, or ad hoc execution;^\[12\]^[^data:45] and material sources are still candidates — the journey log, the week's actual work, the scraped archive, and the wiki.^\[13\]^[^data:45] Amir's account guide records the same 2/4 rhythm, set 2026-08-21, and retired the "avoid posting two days in a row" rule on 2026-08-25 as incompatible with 4 posts a week and without evidence behind it.^\[14\]^[^data:38]^\[15\]^[^data:38]

One observation carries into the research round unadopted: text-only posts have no production dependency and could run as a daily habit that can never be blocked,^\[16\]^[^data:45] while posts needing a video, graphic, or screen recording are production-constrained exactly like reels.^\[17\]^[^data:45]

## Account roles and voices

The Pam company account uses a calm, direct, editorial brand voice.^\[18\]^[^data:39] Amir is the primary content voice: LinkedIn is for community and brand — not leads, not investors, not launch announcements — and for building an audience of founders and operators who feel the problem PAM solves before PAM is ever named.^\[19\]^[^data:15] His four pillars are building in public (what he actually builds: Pam, the Company Brain, automations — specific decisions, what worked, what didn't),^\[20\]^[^data:38] AI-engineering education,^\[21\]^[^data:38] honest signal on AI,^\[22\]^[^data:38] and personal story,^\[23\]^[^data:38] addressed to a non-engineer audience.^\[24\]^[^data:38] His tone is direct, founder-led, technically credible, and free of corporate speak.^\[25\]^[^data:38] His format rules: the opening line is the hook with no preamble,^\[26\]^[^data:38] short paragraphs, single sentences where possible,^\[27\]^[^data:38] and no bullet-point listicles unless the structure genuinely serves the point;^\[28\]^[^data:38] a 150–600-word typical range,^\[29\]^[^data:38] closing on a specific point or question rather than a generic "what do you think?".^\[30\]^[^data:38]

Niyayesh owns content creation and execution across LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube Shorts.^\[31\]^[^data:19] Her PAM LinkedIn lane is an editorial brand voice at 1–3 posts/week rotating across The Problem, The POV, and The Proof.^\[32\]^[^data:19]

Negin's personal LinkedIn is a professional product-side presence broader than Pam: she is a product designer and lead telling the story of how she thinks — not a founder telling the startup's origin story, with Pam as the case study rather than the subject.^\[33\]^[^data:40] Her topics are product design method and judgment illustrated through real decisions, contrarian defensible product takes, positioning and category arguments, and design rationale;^\[34\]^[^data:40] her voice is essayistic and argument-led, not hook-tension-reveal or listicle.^\[35\]^[^data:40] Her voice guide was an empty shell as of 2026-07-04, pending review of her published posts;^\[36\]^[^data:16] the foundation was then built directly with her on 2026-07-17 through a structured interview — she has no reviewed posts yet — and both are to be revisited once real posts and feedback exist.^\[37\]^[^data:40]^\[38\]^[^data:40] The documented target is a first post live by 2026-08-04, locked 2026-07-17.^\[39\]^[^data:40] Her planned drafting rule matches Amir's — she writes, the brain helps her write better — but it has not been tested in real drafting sessions.^\[40\]^[^data:16]

## Drafting, storage, and approval

A post is one row in the Supabase `posts` table, holding state and content,^\[41\]^[^data:22] and the scratch file in `work/drafts/` is ephemeral and deleted on approval.^\[42\]^[^data:39] Keep `posts.content` and `posts.content_brief` current as the source of truth;^\[43\]^[^data:22] ideas come from sessions, the journey material well, or promotion from the wiki, and promoted posts carry `idea_ref` back to their wiki page.^\[44\]^[^data:22]

Drafting is owned by the `linkedin-post-writer` skill, which loads the author's voice, the channel playbook, and searches the wiki before writing — the author's words are the post.^\[45\]^[^data:39] For Amir the rule is assistive, not substitutive: he writes, the brain helps him write better, and never writes a post from scratch.^\[46\]^[^data:15] Concretely: brainstorm and surface angles on request, research when context is needed, take his draft or voice transcript and help craft it, refine with minimal intervention.^\[47\]^[^data:15] Never restructure, never replace his voice with cleaner prose,^\[48\]^[^data:15] and never invent his facts, experiences, or opinion.^\[49\]^[^data:15] The playbook directory grows on demand — no playbook docs exist yet, and one is added only when there is proven LinkedIn-specific craft worth keeping.^\[50\]^[^data:39]

## Platform craft — provisional until the research round

The June platform write-up explicitly labels its sampled observations "historical sample observations, not current algorithm rules."^\[51\]^[^data:227] Until a cycle is designed, the working default for founder/operator posts is native text: June put the band at 200–500 words with a Problem → Insight → Implication arc of roughly 40% / 40% / 20% and backstory under half the post;^\[52\]^[^data:227]^\[53\]^[^data:227]^\[54\]^[^data:227] the August five-cluster run confirms a comment-depth hierarchy of medium prose (150–400 words) over short one-liners over listicles — a comment-engagement signal, not a reach signal.^\[55\]^[^data:227]^\[56\]^[^data:227] Emojis stay at 0–1 per post;^\[57\]^[^data:227] external links are a mixed signal, and the safe default is no external links unless the post is authority-building and the credibility signal is strong;^\[58\]^[^data:227]^\[59\]^[^data:227] carousels were absent from top performers in both June datasets, and no video or carousel appeared in the top tier across all five August clusters — text-only dominates.^\[60\]^[^data:227]^\[61\]^[^data:227]

Specificity is load-bearing: named decisions ("we ditched our RAG setup") outperform abstract takes ("AI governance is important").^\[62\]^[^data:227] Comment-to-reaction ratio is a stronger signal than raw reactions;^\[63\]^[^data:227] concrete, technical content earns more reshares while emotional or aspirational content earns more reactions — know which response to optimize for before writing.^\[64\]^[^data:227] The August run sharpens this into a reach-versus-conversation choice: a genuine, direct question ("what am I missing?" drew an 87% comment ratio) draws far more conversation than a declarative statement of the same length;^\[65\]^[^data:227] "don't you think?" activates debate while rhetorical questions do not — optimize for shares with contradiction, and for conversation with a genuine question or a named decision the reader can weigh in on.^\[66\]^[^data:227]^\[67\]^[^data:227]

Later June observations favor first-person founder voice over advisor/consultant framing at equivalent topic and length, found short teaser-with-link posts near-zero engagement, and found heavy-emoji listicles earning reach while suppressing comments.^\[68\]^[^data:141]^\[69\]^[^data:141]^\[70\]^[^data:141]

The hook guide treats the opening as a job-specific mechanism — curiosity, challenge, proof, surprise, direct pain diagnosis, comparison, compressed authority, or an invitation that makes participation feel easy — with the first line establishing stakes without greetings and the rest of the piece clearing the expectation the hook creates.^\[71\]^[^data:293]^\[72\]^[^data:293] On LinkedIn the hook is gating: if it fails to stop the scroll and earn the "see more" click-through, no amount of body quality saves the post.^\[73\]^[^data:293] Its selection table maps max comment volume to the problem-first open question, reshares to easy/hard contrast or evidence-first essays, authenticity to vulnerability/friction, practitioner authority to the counter-intuitive reframe, and concrete proof to first-person narrative plus outcome.^\[74\]^[^data:293] The guide also documents an invitation hook — a direct exchange like "I'll get the coffee. You tell me what you're building" — where the value, role, and ask are legible before any explanation; use it when the desired action is participation rather than passive attention.^\[75\]^[^data:293]^\[76\]^[^data:293]

The guide also argues hooks matter more on LinkedIn than elsewhere because the feed is comment- and dwell-time driven: a mediocre post with a great hook outperforms a great post with a mediocre hook, so one hook rewrite is worth three body rewrites.^\[77\]^[^data:293]^\[78\]^[^data:293] Two of its named patterns carry explicit fit notes for Amir: the counter-intuitive reframe maps to his observational, practitioner voice — the credible speaker is the one who has been through the build and found the conventional wisdom wrong;^\[79\]^[^data:293] and the easy/hard contrast lands because his practitioner credibility (shipping agents in production) gives him the standing to name the easy part, with a drafted example of "Deploying an AI agent is the easy part. Governing it is where things get interesting."^\[80\]^[^data:293]^\[81\]^[^data:293]

Contradiction is the dominant hook formula across all five August clusters, appearing in top-performing posts across solo-founder, build-in-public, non-technical founder, and honest-signal clusters;^\[82\]^[^data:227]^\[83\]^[^data:227] the June scouts had already identified counter-intuitive reframes, problem-first open questions, first-person narratives with concrete outcomes, vulnerability/friction, and evidence-first essays as recurring patterns.^\[84\]^[^data:139]^\[85\]^[^data:139]^\[86\]^[^data:139]^\[87\]^[^data:139]^\[88\]^[^data:139]

For product posts with native video, text answers why the feature matters while the video proves it exists and behaves specifically; the first frame should establish the product category and the result should be understandable without audio — one observed example, not yet a platform-performance claim.^\[89\]^[^data:227]^\[90\]^[^data:227]^\[91\]^[^data:227]

The August run also narrows audience fit. Build-in-public peaks at personal transformation, not progress updates — generic progress-update posts did not appear in the top tier.^\[92\]^[^data:227]^\[93\]^[^data:227] Top solo-founder posts mostly write about solo founding from the outside rather than from inside a live build, a positioning gap Amir can occupy.^\[94\]^[^data:227]^\[95\]^[^data:227] And the non-technical-founder claim has evolved from "I don't know how to code" to "I'm obsessed with the problem, so I find the tools" — tool access is no longer the frame.^\[96\]^[^data:227]^\[97\]^[^data:227]

A reusable carousel reference uses a structured-document, file-tree aesthetic with clean white backgrounds, a two-column item-name/benefit hierarchy, and strong CTA buttons.^\[98\]^[^data:66] Its fit for non-technical audiences remains an open question.^\[99\]^[^data:66] Because June's datasets found no top-performing carousels^\[100\]^[^data:227] and August's five clusters confirmed text-only dominance,^\[101\]^[^data:227] this visual system is a template experiment rather than a settled default for the founder/operator audience.

The Joerg Storm benchmark is a pattern reference, not a general rule: it records the easy/hard contrast structure, a high-polarity closing question that forces the reader to take a position, and external links included without apparent penalty — his domain authority shields him from LinkedIn's typical link discount, which average accounts cannot assume.^\[102\]^[^data:84]^\[103\]^[^data:84]^\[104\]^[^data:84]

Dissonance is not an adopted LinkedIn rule: its source leaves open whether the framework applies to written content or is inherently a video mechanic, while identifying the text analogue as a contradiction hook that flips a dominant assumption in one opening line.^\[105\]^[^data:106]^\[106\]^[^data:106]

## Topic fit and evidence discipline

The repeated June practitioner question was how to take what works in a demo and make it work when real users hit it;^\[107\]^[^data:140] the named production failure modes — memory loss, concurrent state collapse, verification gates, context constraints — are the specificity signal that gives readers something concrete to recognize or challenge.^\[108\]^[^data:227] The first scout also tracked MCP as an infrastructure layer, enterprise knowledge management versus RAG, and the demo-to-production gap as emerging themes.^\[109\]^[^data:139]^\[110\]^[^data:139]^\[111\]^[^data:139]

The August 31 scout marks the topic shift: production governance has replaced the demo-to-production gap as the AI-agent conversation — monitoring, rollback, policy enforcement, and a trust layer for running systems rather than post-mortems on failed deployments — a signal the audience has moved further along since June.^\[112\]^[^data:271]^\[113\]^[^data:271] Its MCP cluster surfaced a documentation-saturation gap — "everyone ships MCP servers and almost no one describes them" — flagged as a potential first-mover content angle for Amir if he can describe a deployed MCP integration.^\[114\]^[^data:271]^\[115\]^[^data:271] Its consensus frame is judgment over tools: the constraint on AI value is the quality of the human's judgment, domain knowledge, or curiosity rather than model access, which the scout reads as a positioning claim letting Amir claim genuine AI advantage through domain expertise and operating judgment.^\[116\]^[^data:271]^\[117\]^[^data:271]

AI-agent governance is a research lead rather than an adopted claim: Marcel Velica's governance post earned 1,475 reactions and 800 comments, the highest engagement in the batch by 2–3x, but Amir-specific credibility is not documented and a practitioner post would need a concrete deployment incident rather than borrowed enterprise framing.^\[118\]^[^data:101]^\[119\]^[^data:101] The theme is maintained on [AI Agent Governance](../../../ai-practice/ai-agent-governance/page.md). Engagement filters must be paired with relevance filters so off-topic emotional or aspirational posts do not become editorial evidence.^\[120\]^[^data:227]

## Research tooling

The channel's research tool is an open dependency: `linkedin-scout` is assessed as "really badly configured, not that much helpful," and the requirement Amir described is a browsable, persistent, tagged Content Library that agents work over — not a report generated and lost. That is an architecture change, and it belongs to the research round.^\[121\]^[^data:45]^\[122\]^[^data:45] Known failure modes: `scarletapi` returned zero posts across all five June 16 clusters amid seven consecutive zero-yield runs,^\[123\]^[^data:140]^\[124\]^[^data:140] and both actors timed out after about 16 minutes on the June 17 Building with AI cluster — a second consecutive zero-yield run treated as infrastructure, not a topic signal.^\[125\]^[^data:141] The August run confirmed `harvestapi` reliable (4/5 rating across all five clusters, 44–50 posts per run) while `scarletapi` returned zero posts across five query clusters, with a proposal filed to remove it from rotation.^\[126\]^[^data:227]^\[127\]^[^data:227]^\[128\]^[^data:227]
