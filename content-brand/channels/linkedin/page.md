# LinkedIn

Pam's LinkedIn presence is a shared working space for four accounts — the Pam company account plus personal accounts for Amir, Niyayesh, and Negin — with per-account playbooks, one database-backed post pipeline, and a research practice feeding the channel.^\[1\]^ (`data:39`) The execution cycle is deliberately parked as of 2026-08-25: Amir decided to park it (`decision:226`) until a LinkedIn-specific research round runs, because building one now would mean guessing at the mechanics of a platform that has not been studied.^\[2\]^ (`data:45`) Whatever gets built also serves Negin, so this is not only Amir's channel.^\[3\]^ (`data:45`)

For the cross-channel portfolio see [Channels](../page.md); Amir's niche and pillars are maintained on [Founder Content Strategy](../../founder-content-strategy/page.md), shared short-form craft on [Production Craft](../instagram/production-craft/page.md), and the research-corpus architecture on [Content Library](../../../systems/company-brain/content-library/page.md).

## Execution status

The strategy file fixes the channel's decided shape: priority second behind Instagram — whose cycle is designed while LinkedIn's is not —^\[4\]^ (`data:46`) volume at a 2 a week floor and 4 a week target,^\[5\]^ (`data:45`) posts written separate rather than repurposed from Instagram,^\[6\]^ (`data:45`) one lead magnet a week made Mondays and shared with Instagram,^\[7\]^ (`data:45`) and a LinkedIn streak carried separately from Instagram's.^\[8\]^ (`data:45`)

What is open: post days are unset — the streak cannot be built without them;^\[9\]^ (`data:45`) the writing block is unresolved between a daily habit block, a fixed weekly block, or ad hoc execution;^\[10\]^ (`data:45`) and material sources are still candidates — the journey log, the week's actual work, the scraped archive, and the wiki.^\[11\]^ (`data:45`) Amir's account guide records the same 2/4 rhythm, set 2026-08-21, and retired the "avoid posting two days in a row" rule on 2026-08-25 as incompatible with 4 posts a week and without evidence behind it.^\[12\]^ (`data:38`)^\[13\]^ (`data:38`)

One observation carries into the research round unadopted: text-only posts have no production dependency and could run as a daily habit that can never be blocked,^\[14\]^ (`data:45`) while posts needing a video, graphic, or screen recording are production-constrained exactly like reels.^\[15\]^ (`data:45`)

## Account roles and voices

The Pam company account uses a calm, direct, editorial brand voice.^\[16\]^ (`data:39`) Amir is the primary content voice: LinkedIn is for community and brand — not leads, not investors, not launch announcements — and for building an audience of founders and operators who feel the problem PAM solves before PAM is ever named.^\[17\]^ (`data:15`) His four pillars are building in public (what he actually builds: Pam, the Company Brain, automations — specific decisions, what worked, what didn't),^\[18\]^ (`data:38`) AI-engineering education,^\[19\]^ (`data:38`) honest signal on AI,^\[20\]^ (`data:38`) and personal story,^\[21\]^ (`data:38`) addressed to a non-engineer audience.^\[22\]^ (`data:38`) His tone is direct, founder-led, technically credible, and free of corporate speak.^\[23\]^ (`data:38`) His format rules: the opening line is the hook with no preamble,^\[24\]^ (`data:38`) short paragraphs, single sentences where possible,^\[25\]^ (`data:38`) and no bullet-point listicles unless the structure genuinely serves the point;^\[26\]^ (`data:38`) a 150–600-word typical range,^\[27\]^ (`data:38`) closing on a specific point or question rather than a generic "what do you think?".^\[28\]^ (`data:38`)

Niyayesh owns content creation and execution across LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube Shorts.^\[29\]^ (`data:19`) Her PAM LinkedIn lane is an editorial brand voice at 1–3 posts/week rotating across The Problem, The POV, and The Proof.^\[30\]^ (`data:19`)

Negin's personal LinkedIn is a professional product-side presence broader than Pam: she is a product designer and lead telling the story of how she thinks — not a founder telling the startup's origin story, with Pam as the case study rather than the subject.^\[31\]^ (`data:40`) Her topics are product design method and judgment illustrated through real decisions, contrarian defensible product takes, positioning and category arguments, and design rationale;^\[32\]^ (`data:40`) her voice is essayistic and argument-led, not hook-tension-reveal or listicle.^\[33\]^ (`data:40`) Her voice guide was an empty shell as of 2026-07-04, pending review of her published posts;^\[34\]^ (`data:16`) the foundation was then built directly with her on 2026-07-17 through a structured interview — she has no reviewed posts yet — and both are to be revisited once real posts and feedback exist.^\[35\]^ (`data:40`)^\[36\]^ (`data:40`) The documented target is a first post live by 2026-08-04, locked 2026-07-17.^\[37\]^ (`data:40`) Her planned drafting rule matches Amir's — she writes, the brain helps her write better — but it has not been tested in real drafting sessions.^\[38\]^ (`data:16`)

## Drafting, storage, and approval

A post is one row in the Supabase `posts` table, holding state and content,^\[39\]^ (`data:22`) and the scratch file in `work/drafts/` is ephemeral and deleted on approval.^\[40\]^ (`data:39`) Keep `posts.content` and `posts.content_brief` current as the source of truth;^\[41\]^ (`data:22`) ideas come from sessions, the journey material well, or promotion from the wiki, and promoted posts carry `idea_ref` back to their wiki page.^\[42\]^ (`data:22`)

Drafting is owned by the `linkedin-post-writer` skill, which loads the author's voice, the channel playbook, and searches the wiki before writing — the author's words are the post.^\[43\]^ (`data:39`) For Amir the rule is assistive, not substitutive: he writes, the brain helps him write better, and never writes a post from scratch.^\[44\]^ (`data:15`) Concretely: brainstorm and surface angles on request, research when context is needed, take his draft or voice transcript and help craft it, refine with minimal intervention.^\[45\]^ (`data:15`) Never restructure, never replace his voice with cleaner prose,^\[46\]^ (`data:15`) and never invent his facts, experiences, or opinion.^\[47\]^ (`data:15`) The playbook directory grows on demand — no playbook docs exist yet, and one is added only when there is proven LinkedIn-specific craft worth keeping.^\[48\]^ (`data:39`)

## Platform craft — provisional until the research round

The June platform write-up explicitly labels its sampled observations "historical sample observations, not current algorithm rules."^\[49\]^ (`data:227`) Until a cycle is designed, the working default for founder/operator posts is native text: June put the band at 200–500 words with a Problem → Insight → Implication arc of roughly 40% / 40% / 20% and backstory under half the post;^\[50\]^ (`data:227`)^\[51\]^ (`data:227`)^\[52\]^ (`data:227`) the August five-cluster run confirms a comment-depth hierarchy of medium prose (150–400 words) over short one-liners over listicles — a comment-engagement signal, not a reach signal.^\[53\]^ (`data:227`)^\[54\]^ (`data:227`) Emojis stay at 0–1 per post;^\[55\]^ (`data:227`) external links are a mixed signal, and the safe default is no external links unless the post is authority-building and the credibility signal is strong;^\[56\]^ (`data:227`)^\[57\]^ (`data:227`) carousels were absent from top performers in both June datasets, and no video or carousel appeared in the top tier across all five August clusters — text-only dominates.^\[58\]^ (`data:227`)^\[59\]^ (`data:227`)

Specificity is load-bearing: named decisions ("we ditched our RAG setup") outperform abstract takes ("AI governance is important").^\[60\]^ (`data:227`) Comment-to-reaction ratio is a stronger signal than raw reactions;^\[61\]^ (`data:227`) concrete, technical content earns more reshares while emotional or aspirational content earns more reactions — know which response to optimize for before writing.^\[62\]^ (`data:227`) The August run sharpens this into a reach-versus-conversation choice: a genuine, direct question ("what am I missing?" drew an 87% comment ratio) draws far more conversation than a declarative statement of the same length;^\[63\]^ (`data:227`) "don't you think?" activates debate while rhetorical questions do not — optimize for shares with contradiction, and for conversation with a genuine question or a named decision the reader can weigh in on.^\[64\]^ (`data:227`)^\[65\]^ (`data:227`)

Later June observations favor first-person founder voice over advisor/consultant framing at equivalent topic and length, found short teaser-with-link posts near-zero engagement, and found heavy-emoji listicles earning reach while suppressing comments.^\[66\]^ (`data:141`)^\[67\]^ (`data:141`)^\[68\]^ (`data:141`)

The hook guide treats the opening as a job-specific mechanism — curiosity, challenge, proof, surprise, direct pain diagnosis, comparison, compressed authority, or an invitation that makes participation feel easy — with the first line establishing stakes without greetings and the rest of the piece clearing the expectation the hook creates.^\[69\]^ (`data:249`)^\[70\]^ (`data:249`) On LinkedIn the hook is gating: if it fails to stop the scroll and earn the "see more" click-through, no amount of body quality saves the post.^\[71\]^ (`data:249`) Its selection table maps max comment volume to the problem-first open question, reshares to easy/hard contrast or evidence-first essays, authenticity to vulnerability/friction, practitioner authority to the counter-intuitive reframe, and concrete proof to first-person narrative plus outcome.^\[72\]^ (`data:249`) The guide also documents an invitation hook — a direct exchange like "I'll get the coffee. You tell me what you're building" — where the value, role, and ask are legible before any explanation; use it when the desired action is participation rather than passive attention.^\[73\]^ (`data:249`)^\[74\]^ (`data:249`) Contradiction is the dominant hook formula across all five August clusters, appearing in top-performing posts across solo-founder, build-in-public, non-technical founder, and honest-signal clusters;^\[75\]^ (`data:227`)^\[76\]^ (`data:227`) the June scouts had already identified counter-intuitive reframes, problem-first open questions, first-person narratives with concrete outcomes, vulnerability/friction, and evidence-first essays as recurring patterns.^\[77\]^ (`data:139`)^\[78\]^ (`data:139`)^\[79\]^ (`data:139`)^\[80\]^ (`data:139`)^\[81\]^ (`data:139`)

For product posts with native video, text answers why the feature matters while the video proves it exists and behaves specifically; the first frame should establish the product category and the result should be understandable without audio — one observed example, not yet a platform-performance claim.^\[82\]^ (`data:227`)^\[83\]^ (`data:227`)^\[84\]^ (`data:227`)

The August run also narrows audience fit. Build-in-public peaks at personal transformation, not progress updates — generic progress-update posts did not appear in the top tier.^\[85\]^ (`data:227`)^\[86\]^ (`data:227`) Top solo-founder posts mostly write about solo founding from the outside rather than from inside a live build, a positioning gap Amir can occupy.^\[87\]^ (`data:227`)^\[88\]^ (`data:227`) And the non-technical-founder claim has evolved from "I don't know how to code" to "I'm obsessed with the problem, so I find the tools" — tool access is no longer the frame.^\[89\]^ (`data:227`)^\[90\]^ (`data:227`)

A reusable carousel reference uses a structured-document, file-tree aesthetic with clean white backgrounds, a two-column item-name/benefit hierarchy, and strong CTA buttons.^\[91\]^ (`data:66`) Its fit for non-technical audiences remains an open question.^\[92\]^ (`data:66`) Because June's datasets found no top-performing carousels^\[93\]^ (`data:227`) and August's five clusters confirmed text-only dominance,^\[94\]^ (`data:227`) this visual system is a template experiment rather than a settled default for the founder/operator audience.

The Joerg Storm benchmark is a pattern reference, not a general rule: it records the easy/hard contrast structure, a high-polarity closing question that forces the reader to take a position, and external links included without apparent penalty — his domain authority shields him from LinkedIn's typical link discount, which average accounts cannot assume.^\[95\]^ (`data:84`)^\[96\]^ (`data:84`)^\[97\]^ (`data:84`)

Dissonance is not an adopted LinkedIn rule: its source leaves open whether the framework applies to written content or is inherently a video mechanic, while identifying the text analogue as a contradiction hook that flips a dominant assumption in one opening line.^\[98\]^ (`data:106`)^\[99\]^ (`data:106`)

## Topic fit and evidence discipline

The repeated June practitioner question was how to take what works in a demo and make it work when real users hit it;^\[100\]^ (`data:140`) the named production failure modes — memory loss, concurrent state collapse, verification gates, context constraints — are the specificity signal that gives readers something concrete to recognize or challenge.^\[101\]^ (`data:227`) The first scout also tracked MCP as an infrastructure layer, enterprise knowledge management versus RAG, and the demo-to-production gap as emerging themes.^\[102\]^ (`data:139`)^\[103\]^ (`data:139`)^\[104\]^ (`data:139`)

AI-agent governance is a research lead rather than an adopted claim: Marcel Velica's governance post earned 1,475 reactions and 800 comments, the highest engagement in the batch by 2–3x, but Amir-specific credibility is not documented and a practitioner post would need a concrete deployment incident rather than borrowed enterprise framing.^\[105\]^ (`data:101`)^\[106\]^ (`data:101`) The theme is maintained on [AI Agent Governance](../../../ai-practice/ai-agent-governance/page.md). Engagement filters must be paired with relevance filters so off-topic emotional or aspirational posts do not become editorial evidence.^\[107\]^ (`data:227`)

## Research tooling

The channel's research tool is an open dependency: `linkedin-scout` is assessed as "really badly configured, not that much helpful," and the requirement Amir described is a browsable, persistent, tagged Content Library that agents work over — not a report generated and lost. That is an architecture change, and it belongs to the research round.^\[108\]^ (`data:45`)^\[109\]^ (`data:45`) Known failure modes: `scarletapi` returned zero posts across all five June 16 clusters amid seven consecutive zero-yield runs,^\[110\]^ (`data:140`)^\[111\]^ (`data:140`) and both actors timed out after about 16 minutes on the June 17 Building with AI cluster — a second consecutive zero-yield run treated as infrastructure, not a topic signal.^\[112\]^ (`data:141`) The August run confirmed `harvestapi` reliable (4/5 rating across all five clusters, 44–50 posts per run) while `scarletapi` returned zero posts across five query clusters, with a proposal filed to remove it from rotation.^\[113\]^ (`data:227`)^\[114\]^ (`data:227`)^\[115\]^ (`data:227`)
