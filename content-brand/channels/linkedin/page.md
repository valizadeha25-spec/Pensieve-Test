# LinkedIn

PamAI's LinkedIn is a shared working space for the Pam company account and personal accounts for Amir, Niyayesh, and Negin, with one database-backed post pipeline.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/39) The detailed account list names four files, while the README's short description still says “Three accounts”; the inventory is the more specific account record.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/39) LinkedIn is second to Instagram, and its execution cycle was deliberately parked on 2026-08-25 pending a LinkedIn-specific research round.[platform-priority decision](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/209) [parked execution-cycle decision](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/226) [^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45)

## Operating status

The channel-level operating decision sets a 2 a week floor and 4 a week target, keeps LinkedIn separate rather than repurposed from Instagram, shares one weekly lead magnet with Instagram, and gives LinkedIn its own streak.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45) Niyayesh's account brief separately describes PAM LinkedIn as 1–3 posts/week rotating across The Problem, The POV, and The Proof, so the channel target and the account-specific note are not identical.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/19) [^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45)

The parking is explicit: designing a cycle before the research round would mean guessing at unstudied platform mechanics.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45) Post days, writing timing, material sources, and the answer to what actually works remain open.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45) Text-only posts have no production dependency, while posts needing video, graphics, or screen recordings are production-constrained like reels; the strategy marks this split as an observation, not an adopted cycle.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45)

## Accounts and voices

The Pam company account is calm, direct, and editorial.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/39) Amir's stated LinkedIn job is community and brand—not leads, investors, or launch announcements—by building an audience of founders and operators who feel the problem PAM solves before PAM is named.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) His four pillars are building in public, AI-engineering education, honest signal on AI, and personal story, aimed at a non-engineer audience.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/38) His voice is direct, founder-led, technically credible, and free of corporate speak; the opening line is the hook with no preamble, and the close should be a specific point or question rather than generic engagement bait.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/38)

Niyayesh owns content creation and execution across LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube Shorts.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/19) Her PAM LinkedIn lane is an editorial brand voice rotating across The Problem, The POV, and The Proof.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/19)

Negin's lane is a personal professional presence broader than Pam: she is a product designer and lead telling the story of how she thinks, with Pam as the case study rather than the subject.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/40) Her topics are product-design method and judgment, contrarian product takes, positioning and category arguments, and the rationale behind specific decisions; the intended voice is essayistic and argument-led, not hook-tension-reveal or listicle.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/40) Her foundation was built directly with her through a structured interview on 2026-07-17 rather than a review of existing posts, and the playbook says to revisit it after real posts and feedback.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/40)

## Content pipeline and drafting

A LinkedIn post is one row in the Supabase `posts` table holding state and content. `posts.content` and `posts.content_brief` are the source of truth; ideas enter from sessions, the `journey/` material well, or wiki promotion, with promoted posts carrying `idea_ref` back to their wiki page.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/22) The scratch file in `work/drafts/` is ephemeral and deleted on approval.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/39)

Drafting belongs to the `linkedin-post-writer` skill, which loads the author's voice, the channel playbook, and the wiki before writing; the author's words remain the post.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/39) For Amir, the helper can brainstorm, research, and lightly refine a draft or voice transcript, but must not restructure it, replace his voice, or invent his facts, experiences, or opinions.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) Negin's equivalent rule—she writes and the brain helps her write better—was explicitly not yet tested in real drafting sessions.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16) The LinkedIn playbook grows only when proven LinkedIn-specific craft warrants a durable note.[^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/39)

## Provisional platform craft

The platform reference labels its sampled mechanics “historical sample observations, not current algorithm rules.”[^\[25\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227) Its founder/operator baseline is 200–500 words in a Problem → Insight → Implication arc of roughly 40% / 40% / 20%, with backstory below half the post.[^\[26\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227) The August 2026 sample found medium prose (150–400 words) ahead of short one-liners and listicles for comment depth, while warning that this is a comment-engagement signal rather than a reach signal.[^\[27\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227)

Comment-to-reaction ratio is more useful than raw reactions for judging conversation; concrete, specific technical content tends to earn reshares, while emotional or aspirational content tends to earn reactions.[^\[28\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227) For the intended response, the working choice is a genuine question or named decision for conversation and contradiction for shares.[^\[29\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227) Specificity is the credibility separator: named decisions such as “we ditched our RAG setup” outperform abstract takes such as “AI governance is important.”[^\[30\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227) The hygiene defaults are 0–1 emoji with no opener emoji, and no external link unless the post is authority-building and the credibility signal is strong.[^\[31\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227)

The August sample supports native text as the working default: no video or carousel appeared in the top tier across all five clusters, except an MCP Apps demo where text set up the workflow and video proved it.[^\[32\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227) A structured-document carousel remains a template experiment—file-tree or directory-listing layout, clean white space, two columns for item and benefit, and a strong CTA—but its fit for non-technical audiences is still open.[^\[33\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/66) [^\[34\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/66) For a product demo, text explains why the feature matters while video proves that it exists and behaves specifically; the first frame should establish the product category and the result should work without audio. This is an observed format, not a platform-performance claim.[^\[35\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227)

## Hooks and audience fit

The hook library treats an opening as a content-job mechanism—curiosity, challenge, proof, surprise, pain diagnosis, comparison, compressed authority, or participation—not merely as a sentence. The first line or frame should establish stakes without greetings or scene-setting, and the body must clear the expectation the hook creates.[^\[36\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/314) On LinkedIn the hook is a gate: if it does not stop the scroll and earn the “see more” click-through, body quality cannot rescue the post.[^\[37\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/314)

Contradiction was the dominant hook formula across all five August clusters.[^\[38\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227) The selection map pairs problem-first open questions with comment volume, easy/hard contrast or evidence-first essays with reshares, vulnerability/friction with authenticity, counter-intuitive reframes with practitioner authority, and first-person narrative plus outcome with concrete proof.[^\[39\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/314) The easy/hard form names the widely adopted step and then the overlooked hard step; its fit for Amir depends on his practitioner credibility shipping agents in production.[^\[40\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/84)

The new niche-filter pattern names a narrow audience before a recurring fear, accepts the fear rather than rejecting it, and turns it into a practical positioning choice such as moving from a broad tool toward an industry's nuanced workflows.[^\[41\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/314) It belongs only where the audience welcomes exclusion and shares the fear; the next beat must make an actionable choice, or the filter becomes empty gatekeeping.[^\[42\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/314)

The August audience read favours identity and transformation over generic milestone updates in build-in-public content, lived inside-the-build experience over outside analysis in solo-founder content, and problem obsession over “I can't code, but I use tools” for non-technical founders.[^\[43\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271) Across clusters, it frames human judgment, domain knowledge, and curiosity—not model quality or tool access—as the constraint on getting value from AI.[^\[44\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271) It also records a shift from the June demo-to-production question toward governing deployed agents through monitoring, rollback, policy enforcement, and a trust layer, plus an MCP documentation gap for practitioners with a deployed integration.[^\[45\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/271)

These are research leads, not permission to borrow claims: the June governance angle requires a concrete deployment incident before it can become an authoritative Amir post.[^\[46\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/101) Engagement filters also need to be paired with relevance filters for topic-specific scouting.[^\[47\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/101)

## Research tooling

`linkedin-scout` remains an open dependency: Amir described it as “really badly configured, not that much helpful,” while the desired replacement is the Content Library shape—a browsable, persistent, tagged corpus that agents can search and synthesise against rather than a report that is generated and lost.[^\[48\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/45) The August run rated `harvestapi` reliable at 4/5 across five clusters with 44–50 posts per run, while `scarletapi` returned zero posts across all five clusters and was treated as a systemic scraper failure rather than a query signal.[^\[49\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/227)
