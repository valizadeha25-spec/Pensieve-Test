# Twitter

Twitter/X is a documented but currently parked channel: the channel README marks it “PARKED 2026-08-25” and says it is not in the current plan.^\[1\]^[^data:48] The retained account playbook says its cadence is suspended and its underlying thinking is kept for reconsideration rather than treated as a live commitment.^\[2\]^[^data:47]

## Account position

The current presence is a single personal track for Amir, independent of Pam’s company timeline; the account list records it as active since June 2026, while Pam’s company account has not launched.^\[3\]^[^data:48]

At the cross-channel level, Niyayesh owns content creation and execution across Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts.^\[4\]^[^data:19] The same profile also lists “Twitter — PAM’s account, active from Phase 2 onward.”^\[5\]^[^data:19] That planning line conflicts with the channel-specific decision dated 2026-08-25, which parks Twitter and says the Pam company account is not launched; the dated channel-specific status is the current operating position.^\[6\]^[^data:48]

## Operating model

Pam’s content domain is channel-first: each platform is a self-contained world with its own accounts, playbook, and working space.^\[7\]^[^data:22] Twitter’s directory contains `README.md`, `amir.md`, and `work/drafts/` for ephemeral scratch; posts live in the database, and draft scratch is deleted on approval.^\[8\]^[^data:48]

Playbook documentation grows on demand rather than speculatively: there is no Twitter `playbook/` material yet, and it should be added only when there is real Twitter-specific craft to record.^\[9\]^[^data:48]

## Native playbook if resumed

The intended format mix is sharp one-line reactions and hot takes, threads for ideas that need unpacking, and build-in-public updates. The playbook treats these as native X choices rather than a LinkedIn-shaped middle format.^\[10\]^[^data:47] Automated LinkedIn-to-X repurposing is deferred: it would push LinkedIn-shaped content into a format that punishes it and skip learning the platform.^\[11\]^[^data:47]

Voice guidance carries over hook discipline and specificity over abstraction, while dropping LinkedIn’s 900-1,200-character target, Problem→Insight→Implication arc, and “always deliver 3 hook options” ritual.^\[12\]^[^data:47]

Content capture is event-led. The triggers are a reversed decision, work taking 10x longer than expected, a tool or technique solving something cleanly, or a disagreement being resolved; when one occurs, the rule is to put one line in phone notes immediately, then ask at day’s end, “what’s one thing from today I’d tell another founder about?”^\[13\]^[^data:47]

The former rhythm was daily or near-daily posting paired with 2–3 replies to AI/builder accounts on each post day. It is suspended because the plan judged daily posting plus replies not credible alongside five Instagram reels and 2–4 LinkedIn posts a week, and it must not be counted as a current commitment.^\[14\]^[^data:47]

## Evolution boundary

If Pam launches a company account, the channel structure calls for adding `pam.md` and updating the account list. Until then, the single-account parked model and on-demand playbook rule remain the boundary.^\[15\]^[^data:48]
