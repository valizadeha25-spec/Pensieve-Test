# Emma Narrative Account

Emma is a separate, unbranded Instagram narrative account built from anonymized real events. Its connection to Pam stays private: it is not publicly connected to Pam, Negin, or PamAI, and it is not a marketing vehicle for Pam.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24) The story follows a fictionalized person who built something real and does not know what comes next; its central question is whether Emma can find her way through that confusion, not whether the market wants the product.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

## Account boundary and origin

The account was built live with Negin on 2026-08-23. At that point it was not yet named or handled and had not begun publishing.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24) It came out of a real strategic-planning session about what was next for Pam, but the narrative uses the internal experience rather than the investor-facing record: the initial material was cold LinkedIn outreach involving 100 messages, 15 replies, and no committed testers.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

The account keeps the business question outside the story. Emma's outreach attempts are evidence inside the narrative of trying to move through uncertainty, rather than a show about Pam's market question or a public account for reporting traction.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

## Character, tone, and emotional design

Emma is a placeholder name that Negin can change. Her identity is fictionalized and anonymized while the underlying events are real; she is intended to feel vulnerable, human, and specific, not witty or self-aware about being watched.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

The reference point is “Emily in Paris, not Bridgerton”: close and warm rather than observationally witty. The voice should not use an omniscient narrator performing cleverness or dramatic irony; the audience should feel what Emma feels in the moment. A straight documentary voice and then a Lady Whistledown-style all-seeing observer voice were rejected as too solemn or too distant, leaving the close, vulnerable register as the chosen direction.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

Every episode is designed to create two responses at once: recognition from someone who has moved past the feeling and identification from someone living it now.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24) The craft rule is to stay with physical, sensory, present-moment detail—what Emma is doing and feeling in her body—rather than explaining the experience retrospectively or analytically.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

## Series structure and production model

The series gets the audience to know Emma before it introduces plot. Episode 0 is character work about feeling stuck, with no plot event; the first real storyline begins only after Emma exists as someone the audience already cares about.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

The recurring format is three approximately one-minute episodes per week. Each weekly set follows one real week, one real task, and one real result, paced as hook, attempt, and reveal rather than as three unrelated topics.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24) Perplexity was confirmed on 2026-08-23 as able to generate 8-second video clips with audio, so one approximately 60-second episode is planned as roughly 7–8 stitched beats, each carrying a line or moment of narration.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24) The production handoff is manual: Claude writes the script and beat breakdown, while Negin or Comet generates and posts the video; there is no live API connection between the working context and Perplexity.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

## Source-of-truth workflow

Negin supplies the raw material for each week—what actually happened, without needing to shape it. Claude turns that material into the plotted episode and checks it against the two-feelings rule and the chosen tone, but does not invent plot events: Negin's real experience is the only source of what happens to Emma.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

This keeps the account's fictionalization at the level of identity, framing, and craft rather than fabricated events. The first arc is drawn from the real outreach attempt, while the story's emotional question remains Emma's uncertainty about what to do next.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

## Initial episode arc

The initial sequence moves from the disorientation after finishing a real project into the risk of asking other people about it, then leaves the meaning of the response unresolved.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

- **Episode 0 — “Before”:** The pilot establishes the morning after finishing: Emma has a working, real thing but nothing left to build, and sits with feeling unfinished herself. It is a character-establishing episode with no plot event.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)
- **Episode 1 — “The Question”:** Emma realizes that building the thing was not the end. She writes the first outreach message, reads it four times, sends it, and repeats the act 99 more times that week.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)
- **Episode 2 — “The Asking”:** The episode stays with the quiet after sending. Emma sends the hundredth message at 11:40pm, alone and still unsure whether any of it matters.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)
- **Episode 3 — “The Verdict”:** Fifteen people write back and 85 do not. Emma reads the replies twice, interprets the silence personally as well as commercially, and ends without pretending to have an answer; the next question is whether any of the 15 responses were real.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)

## Unresolved publishing decisions

Three decisions remain open in the captured plan:

- The account handle or name has not been decided.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)
- A second pilot beat about what Emma built and why she could not stop until it was real was proposed to deepen the “getting to know Emma” phase, but Negin has not confirmed it.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)
- It is not yet decided whether episodes will become tracked `posts` rows under the two-substrate rule. That publishing workflow is to be revisited when the account is ready to go out, not during creative development.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/24)
