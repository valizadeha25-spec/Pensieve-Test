# Amir's Account

The account-specific plan makes Instagram the primary audience-building channel for Amir.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) It describes this account as personal brand, thought leadership, and build-in-public documentation of the journey, rawer and more in-the-moment than the Pam company account, with Amir speaking rather than a brand account.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) The file says it was rewritten on 2026-08-25 to match decisions from the 2026-08-21 and 2026-08-25 sessions.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Amir’s core profile still labels LinkedIn his primary channel.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) The accompanying personal-channel strategy marks Instagram primary[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) and LinkedIn second.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) This page follows the account-specific Instagram plan for this account; the conflicting LinkedIn label remains a profile-level inconsistency.

## Audience and pillars

The account speaks to two audiences:

- Ambitious, often young people without a traditional computer-science background who want to build with AI.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)
- Business owners, freelancers, managers, and employees who want AI productivity but feel locked out.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

It is explicitly not positioned for engineers or the technical crowd.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

The editorial pillars are:

- **Building in public:** what Amir actually builds—Pam, the Company Brain, automations, and experiments—plus the experience, lessons, failures, emotions, and motivations behind it.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)
- **Education on AI engineering:** frameworks, insights, breakdowns, explanations of why and how to do something, comparisons, and the clone format.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)
- **Honest signal on AI:** no-filter opinions on use cases, the future, and what others are doing.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)
- **Personal story:** viral trends, day-in-the-life material, and low-effort TikTok-style pieces.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

Pillar weights are not yet set.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) The guardrail for building-in-public and inspiration content is evidence: show the specific thing, what it actually took, and what did not work, rather than making a generic claim that anything is possible with AI.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

## Reel vessel and format tiers

The opening vessel is Reels only; Stories and carousels are deliberately out until Reels have been consistent for a few weeks.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Format comes before topic: starting with the topic causes shoot-day paralysis.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

- **Backbone:** talking-head yapping and build-in-public storytelling. These are the two main formats and should make up most of the output.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)
- **Reach experiments:** greenscreen, voiceover, multitasking, clone, tutorial, and presentations. Rotate them deliberately to find what else works and keep the account from reading as one thing.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)
- **Very low effort:** B-roll with text on top and viral trends, which can also be educational. This is the anti-miss valve when the pipeline is empty and a post day is approaching.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

## Cadence and production rhythm

The plan sets five reels a week on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Sunday.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) Monday is a full working day for editing what was shot the previous Thursday and Saturday, ideating that week’s ideas, and making the lead magnet.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) Thursday and Saturday are half-day shoots.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46)

The cycle has a one-week rolling lag: Monday edits the previous week’s footage while ideating for the current week, and that edit session produces five reels for Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Sunday, and the following Monday.[^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) The plan separates a strategic floor of three reels from a streak floor of five; three is a bad week rather than a collapsed system, while all five post days count toward the daily streak commitment.[^\[25\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46)[^\[26\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46)

## Shoot preparation and ownership

A topic alone is not shoot-ready; nothing gets shot from a topic.[^\[27\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46) Every idea needs six fields with no exceptions: `format`, `main insight`, `hook`, `storyline`, `retention`, and `visual plan`.[^\[28\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) `board-ideation` is the default path from saved reels to specified ideas, with `playbook/shoot-prep.md` as the manual fallback.[^\[29\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

For spoken video, `storyline` is an ordered list of story beats. Each beat has a short name and one concise paragraph of the exact words to say on camera; overview-only talking points are not ready to shoot.[^\[30\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/46)

Amir supplies the journey and build-in-public raw material—the what, why, and lesson—when Niyayesh asks; he writes the one-sentence main insight for each chosen idea and leads the storyline pass.[^\[31\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) He posts to his own personal Instagram account.[^\[32\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) The collaboration rule is that Amir writes and collaborators help him write better rather than writing a post from scratch.[^\[33\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) Video scripts preserve the story-beat structure and use concise exact spoken words from Amir’s supplied substance; they must not become overview-only talking points or invent his facts, experiences, or opinions.[^\[34\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15)

## Voice and framing

The voice is direct, raw, and personal.[^\[35\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) It is first-person and Amir’s perspective rather than Pam’s brand voice; there is no polish or retrospective reframe, opinions are stated plainly, and captions stay short because the video does the work.[^\[36\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)[^\[37\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)[^\[38\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)[^\[39\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

Address one person and use “you” singular; never “you guys,” “everyone,” or “you all.”[^\[40\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Choose conviction over charisma: lower energy, no rushing, and pauses are fine.[^\[41\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Never manufacture a reversal; if a hook needs a claim Amir did not make, the real insight has not been found.[^\[42\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Never invent his numbers.[^\[43\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

Every piece must land for someone who has never seen him within the first three seconds. Open on a problem the core, casual, and new audience all feel, then close on something tactical that pays off for the core.[^\[44\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Building-in-public pieces are especially prone to opening on an internal system; they should open on the problem the thing solves, never on the thing itself.[^\[45\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

Pam may be named. The old Phase 1 rule forbidding any mention of Pam is retired; it belonged to a phase that ended 2026-07-15, and pillar 1 is explicitly a lot of Pam content.[^\[46\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) The remaining framing rule is to lead with the problem and lived experience rather than the product: build-in-public content is about the work and the lesson, not a demo or sales pitch.[^\[47\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)

## Capture habit

Record and photograph working moments—meetings, decisions, locations, screens, and reactions—put everything into one shared folder immediately, and do not sort it later.[^\[48\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)[^\[49\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) When there is no camera, leave a thirty-second voice memo about what happened and why it matters; that becomes the structure.[^\[50\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25) Captured material goes to `../journey/`.[^\[51\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/25)
