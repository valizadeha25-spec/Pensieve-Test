# Production Craft

Pam’s Instagram production craft is the durable “why” behind short-form moves, not a workflow to walk by hand. It sits between an idea and a shoot, where the brief’s format, hook, storyline, retention, and visual plan become explicit.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/27) Account-level strategy lives in [Pam Account](../pam-account/page.md) and [Amir's Account](../amir-s-account/page.md); idea development lives in [Content Development](../../../content-development/page.md).

## Brief and axes

The default route from saved reels to fully specified ideas is `board-ideation`: it decomposes a board into a measured parts-bin, grounds recombinations in first-party truth, and graduates keepers into the pipeline with provenance. `shoot-prep.md` is the manual fallback when there is no board to decompose or a single reel needs a deep craft pass.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/27) A complete brief has six fields—format, main insight, hook, storyline, retention, and a visual plan for shooting, editing, and location—and the standing rule is [the six-field no-vague-topic shoot rule](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/210).[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/27)

Keep the axes separate: the vessel is the platform container, the format is the content style, and the structure is the internal pacing; one piece can be a reel shot as greenscreen using an expert-debunk structure.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231) No format is universally best; choose against the niche, available production effort, and desired speed of result.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/231)

## Hook design

A hook can be verbal, visual, physical, or sequenced across the first few beats. Before scripting, give the layers different jobs: the visual layer establishes the literal proof object or action, the written layer makes the frame scannable, and the verbal line supplies tension or promise.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/314) Keep the spoken hook to two sentences maximum and put the tension in the second sentence or the second half of the first.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/312) The current playbook treats its 13 numbered patterns as structural moves to attempt, not guaranteed formulas: run them against the spine and choose the one that creates the sharpest gap.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/312)

For a narrow audience with a real shared fear, an explicit niche filter can open a feared-question reframe: name the audience before its fear so specificity becomes the open loop rather than a limitation.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/312) The next beat must make an actionable choice; otherwise the filter is empty gatekeeping and the threat remains generic anxiety.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/314) Retention devices are downstream of the hook: they hold attention after the opening has worked rather than replacing it.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/312)

## Storyline and retention

Before choosing a storyline or drafting a line, draw the emotional arc. The target is a roller coaster—tension rises, releases briefly, rises again, and earns the payoff—not a flat line or one late spike.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/313) Choose the container before scripting and preserve its beats as touch-points; the final script is a speaking base, not text to memorize.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/313)

Retention is structural: start near the action, deliver value early, release briefly, then create another useful peak. Each beat should advance diagnosis, method, example, or application.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/315) Framework teaching, case-study analysis, workflow demos, monologues, contrast sequences, episodic continuations, and compressed advice stacks need different containers.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/315) For AI workflow reels, preserve the human decision gate: deployment and testing are not proof by themselves, and the next phase starts only after the operator approves the prior one.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/315)

A founder talking-head variant starts with an explicit niche filter, a familiar threat question, and rejection of comforting denial before turning the threat into a concrete positioning choice around vertical workflow depth.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/313) Use it when a feared future-state question can become an immediate operating or positioning decision; without market evidence, the claimed outcome remains a hypothesis rather than proof that vertical software is durable.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/315)

## Visual proof and design

The governing prep question is what the viewer needs to see to believe the claim. For Pam product claims, screen capture is close to mandatory; abstract claims about AI or workflow improvements without visual proof read as unsubstantiated.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/28) Overlay text is a support layer for a number, label, or name, never the proof itself.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/28) Choose the proof object before polishing the script: the strongest sequence usually follows input, mechanism, and result. A literal work artifact can establish that work is happening, but cannot independently verify revenue, valuation, or a promised result.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/308)

Give each claim its own screen, card, or system state and return to the decision or review surface; plan roughly one evidence state every five to eight seconds. Metrics and integrations remain staged examples until real evidence is shown.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/308) Keep the visual system plain and flat—opaque surfaces, thin borders, strong typography, and negative space—and avoid glow, drop shadow, blurred halo, backdrop blur, translucency, glassmorphism, and simulated floating depth.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/28) In a relatable work meme, let the caption carry the condition and reserve the reaction clip for the emotional payoff; do not treat the format as evidence for a product, productivity, or audience-performance claim.[^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/308)

## Audio and shoot gate

Choose music by content job or emotional register, then audition within that smaller bucket. Treat performance language as packaging rather than causal evidence, and verify the track’s license and the platform/account restrictions for the actual post before publishing.[^\[25\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/80)

A reel reaches the shoot gate when the six-field brief and visual plan are complete, including shooting, editing, and location.[^\[26\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/27) Keep the final script as a speaking base rather than memorized text.[^\[27\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/313)
