# Pam Account

Pam’s Instagram is the company’s brand account, built for reach and recognition through participation in platform culture: trends, humour, visual storytelling, and relatable moments rather than polished corporate performance.^\[1\]^ (`data:26`) It is distinct from [Amir's Account](../amir-s-account/page.md); Amir’s personal presence and build-in-public content belongs there.^\[2\]^ (`data:26`) Shared short-form craft is maintained in [Production Craft](../production-craft/page.md), while this page records Pam-specific editorial and operating choices.

## Editorial pillars

- **Trend-riding.** Reels use trending audio, formats, or meme structures, remixed around founder experience, work chaos, or the gap between plans and reality.^\[3\]^ (`data:26`)
- **Founder reality, from the brand angle.** Pam captures the lived texture of building — the 3pm moment, the tab spiral, or a day that looked productive but moved nothing — as a brand perspective rather than a personal diary.^\[4\]^ (`data:26`)
- **Product tease.** The product appears naturally in context: a decision Pam made, a workflow it replaced, or a problem it solved. The content is not an advertisement and does not make the product the subject by default.^\[5\]^ (`data:26`)

## Formats and voice

The current Instagram vessel scope is Reels only; Stories and carousels are deliberately deferred until Reels have been consistent for a few weeks.^\[6\]^ (`data:23`) The earlier Pam account strategy, identified as the May 2026 Instagram Content Strategy and filed 2026-06-13, listed Reels as the primary growth engine, Carousels as secondary for ideas needing more than one frame, and Stories as an always-on informal layer.^\[7\]^ (`data:26`) Those broader entries are historical account guidance, not the current opening scope.^\[8\]^ (`data:23`)

The voice is dry, funny, and self-aware: culturally fluent without trying too hard, relatable before brand, and carried by short captions because the Reel does the work.^\[9\]^ (`data:26`) Pam should not read as corporate Instagram, inspirational-quote content, or a clone of [Amir's Account](../amir-s-account/page.md).^\[10\]^ (`data:26`)

## Production and publishing

Niyayesh owns the Pam account’s publishing pipeline. The 2026-06-30 agreement assigns her the weekly idea-list synthesis, format selection, visual planning, scheduling, and posting to the PAM professional Instagram account; once a piece has its main insight, she owns end-to-end execution.^\[11\]^ (`data:19`)

The May 2026 account strategy sets a batch rhythm coordinated with Amir’s account but using different content: Saturday is for recording and shooting the coming week, Sunday is rest or a backup shoot, weekdays are for editing and posting 2–3 pieces while planning the next weekend, and by Friday the next topics, hooks, and formats are locked.^\[12\]^ (`data:26`)
