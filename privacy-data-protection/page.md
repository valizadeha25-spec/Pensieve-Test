# Privacy & Data Protection

PamAI’s privacy governance is documented in Privacy Policy version 1.0, effective 1 July 2026.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9) PamAI is operated by Amirmohammad Valizadehmehrabani, who is identified as the controller of personal data processed by PamAI.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9) The policy names Amirmohammad Valizadehmehrabani as the data-protection contact and lists valizadeha25@gmail.com for contact.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

## Collection and use

PamAI processes account data; workspace and project data; conversations; files and agent memory; integration data when connected; billing data; operational data; and AI-processing data.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

Data is collected directly from users, from other PamAI users who invite you to workspaces or projects, from connected third-party services, from payment/billing providers, and from automatic service logs. Waitlist and early-access contact details are collected on consent, used only to contact you about access to PamAI, and deleted when no longer needed for that purpose or on request.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

PamAI uses personal data to provide the product and agent, store and retrieve workspaces, projects, messages, files, and memory, run requested integrations and imports, process subscriptions and billing, secure and debug the service, prevent abuse, keep audit records, and meet legal, accounting, tax, and regulatory duties.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9) It does not receive or store full card numbers, CVCs, or bank details; payment details are handled by Polar as merchant of record and Stripe as its payment processor.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

## Legal bases and AI processing

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the stated legal bases are contract, legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligation. Contract covers requested account, workspace, project, agent, integration, billing, support, export, and deletion features. Legitimate interests cover security, abuse prevention, debugging, reliability, and operational quality in ways that do not override rights; training AI models on identifiable content is excluded from that basis and requires explicit opt-in. Consent covers explicit choices such as AI-provider training on interactive conversation data and optional integrations, while legal obligation covers required tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, and compliance records.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

PamAI sends user content to AI models through the Vercel AI Gateway. By default, requests use Zero Data Retention: providers do not retain prompts or outputs after the request is served or use them to train their models. Provider pinning restricts inference to a vetted allowlist of US/EU or Western/GDPR-aligned hosts; requests fail rather than routing outside it, and PamAI does not route user data to first-party China AI endpoints. Users can explicitly opt in through a profile setting to provider training on interactive conversation data; the setting is off by default, and background and system jobs remain Zero Data Retention even when a user opts in for interactive conversations.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

Related technical detail: [Systems](../systems/page.md).

## Third parties, connected data, and transfers

The public sub-processor list includes hosting, storage, authentication, AI inference, messaging, email, sandbox compute, error monitoring, and payment providers.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

When a Google account is connected, PamAI accesses only the Google data needed for requested features, such as basic profile and email for authentication and calendar events for scheduling. The connection, selected calendar metadata, calendar-derived planning entries, and encrypted OAuth tokens are kept only as long as needed for connected features or legal, security, or abuse-prevention obligations; OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest, Google data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS, and access is limited to authorized systems and personnel.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9) Google API data is handled under the Google API Services User Data Policy’s Limited Use requirements: it is used only for requested user-facing features, not advertising, sale, or development, improvement, or training of generalized or non-personalized AI/ML models. Humans do not read Google user data unless the user consents, security or legal compliance requires it, or the data is aggregated/anonymized for internal operations consistent with Limited Use.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9) Disconnecting Google revokes access going forward, stops new calendar sync, deletes or deactivates stored OAuth tokens, and deletes imported calendar entries where technically possible; user-created workspace content that referenced calendar context may remain under the retention rules.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

PamAI and its providers may process data in the United States and other countries; international transfers rely on mechanisms such as data processing agreements, standard contractual clauses, or adequacy decisions where relevant. PamAI is currently offered to users outside the European Union and European Economic Area and is not targeted or intended for individuals located in the EU/EEA; availability in the United Kingdom is assessed separately.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9) PamAI is operated by an individual established in Turkey, so Turkey’s KVKK (Law No. 6698) applies. Turkish users may ask whether their data is processed, request information, correction, or deletion, and object to results produced solely by automated processing; they can use the export/deletion features or contact valizadeha25@gmail.com, and transfers to providers outside Turkey use bases permitted under KVKK.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

## Retention, rights, and disclosures

Account, project, chat, file, and memory data are kept while needed to provide the service. Deleting an account deletes the user’s personal workspace and the projects/files in it; in shared workspaces owned by someone else, PamAI removes the user’s memberships and user-scoped files but may keep shared content belonging to other users. Financial and accounting records are retained for 6 years after account deletion, with personal data minimized or anonymized where legally and technically possible.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, export, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of their personal data. Export and account deletion can be requested through PamAI or by contacting valizadeha25@gmail.com, although some data may be retained for legal, tax, accounting, security, or dispute purposes.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

PamAI does not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. California residents may have rights to know/access, delete, correct, receive a copy of, and opt out of certain uses of personal information, and PamAI will not discriminate against users for exercising privacy rights. It also says it does not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes requiring a California “limit the use of sensitive personal information” link.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

Operational logs and error reports support security and debugging; known email, phone, token, and short content-preview leak sites are redacted, and AI prompt/output trace content is omitted by default unless controlled diagnostic mode is explicitly enabled. PamAI does not use third-party product analytics or tracking scripts.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9) PamAI uses essential cookies only, not non-essential product analytics cookies, so no cookie-consent banner is used; if it adds analytics, session replay, advertising pixels, or tag managers, it will add consent and update the policy first.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)

PamAI is for users aged 16 or older and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. PamAI may update this policy when the product, providers, legal requirements, or data practices change; material changes are reflected with an updated date and appropriate notice.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9)
