# Demand Validation

Demand validation is PamAI’s repeatable decision loop for testing acquisition routes against real commitment, not just attention. The operating rule is to try one real thing, capture one real result, and decide the next experiment rather than run several channels in parallel.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

The documented 90-day plan was built with Negin on 2026-08-23 and scheduled execution to start on 2026-08-24.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

## Validation standard

Replies and signups are curiosity signals, not evidence of willingness to pay.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59) PamAI counts real validation when someone commits time through a real pilot or money through a deposit, LOI, or pre-payment—not when they offer a warm word.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

As of 2026-08-23, cold LinkedIn outreach had produced 100 messages sent, 15 replies, and zero committed testers.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59) The plan says the reply rate is not the problem: it is evidence the message and list are working better than most, while the open question is whether the 15 replies are real signal or “happy ears”.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

## Experiment sequence

Testing is sequential. Week 1 uses direct asks to people who already know you, asking whether the problem lands and whether they know anyone who has it.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59) Week 2 uses one place where the actual ICP gathers; the practice is to be useful and answer real questions without pitching before asking for anything.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

After Week 2, the channel that produced real commitment—not politeness—gets Week 3 onward; the others are dropped or revisited instead of being run in parallel indefinitely.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59) A channel gets a second week only when the first week shows real signal: a commitment, not a kind word.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

When outbound is used for learning, the broad learning motion uses segmented, repeatable outreach to test which personas, pains, triggers, and calls to action produce replies; account-specific research is reserved for high-value targets where one meeting could materially matter.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) A third, offline motion creates a lightweight public reason for strangers to self-select into conversation: Negin offers coffee, names builders broadly, removes sales pressure, and scripts the first line.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) Its learning unit is not a booked sales call but a short conversation with someone actively building or curious enough to approach — so what this motion validates is self-selection, not meeting volume.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) The concrete instance is The One With Coffee: Negin's low-stakes public coffee chat in Brighton, where strangers who find a cafe sign are invited to a 10-15 minute no-pitch conversation about what they are building.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/274) It is deliberately a relationship-discovery surface rather than a narrow tester-recruitment funnel, and the planning guidance is to preserve that no-pressure premise before adding capture, follow-up, or a content layer, since over-instrumenting it early is the named risk.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/274) Whether it should primarily serve personal positioning, product discovery, local community, or content is an open planning question.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/274)

## Weekly measurement

The plan is an active experiment log updated weekly as real results come in.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59) The network strategy optimizes V1 for conversations and tests rather than impressions or raw connection count.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57)

For outbound, open rate asks whether the subject and sender are credible enough, reply rate whether the problem resonates, and meeting-booked rate whether the target persona is real and urgent enough.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) Replies and objections are market feedback, not just a meeting tally.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253)

## Intake surfaces as market signal

Demand signal also lives on the receiving side. The FoundersEdge accelerator application categorizes inbound source paths — accelerator or founder community, angel group, team member, in-person event, mutual connection or referral, social/online, web search or AI recommendation, and other — naming the routes a receiving organization already expects to attribute.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) That makes the intake design itself a market-signal source: match the outbound motion to the route rather than spam every channel — warm referral when possible, founder-community context when credible, and social or web presence that makes a cold application or intro easier to understand.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) The same intake requires a pitch deck and asks for build evidence, customer conversations, recent shipping, and team details, so founder-led outreach should bring proof early rather than relying on biography.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253)

## 90-day decision arc

- Days 1–30: try genuinely different things and find real signal; warm network and then community are evaluated against cold outreach’s existing data.[^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)
- Days 31–60: go deep on the one that worked and track it properly.[^\[25\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)
- Days 61–90: decide from real evidence whether the channel is repeatable or the ICP and offer need to change.[^\[26\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

## Initial test boundary

The initial tester-recruitment focus is English-speaking people personally carrying project execution, prioritizing the UK, UAE, US, Canada, Australia, and Western Europe. It starts with organizations around 1–20 people where the potential tester is close to decisions, lacks dedicated project-management capacity, and has a live project suitable for a 7–10-day test.[^\[27\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57) Titles such as founder or operator are retrieval handles, not qualification.[^\[28\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57)

For the network-led V1 test, PamAI uses LinkedIn connections only, with a maximum of 10 ordinary requests per day. Amir owns all messages and personalized invitation notes; email, Pam-Leads, CommentScout, and automated message sending are excluded.[^\[29\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57)
