# Go-to-Market

PamAI’s go-to-market is a founder-led, relationship-led learning system.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57) It seeks qualified relationships with people likely to test PAM, teach the team about project-execution problems, refer relevant users, or become advocates; V1 optimizes for conversations and tests rather than impressions or raw connection count.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57)

## Market definition and position

PamAI’s market position starts from the management role rather than another software category. Pam is described as a judgment-centered conversational intelligence that runs the project-management cycle on the user’s behalf, while its sharper category claim is a decision layer that tells the user which task is actually worth doing today instead of another tool to execute.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/20) [^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/72)

The working primary ICP hypothesis is early-stage founders and small teams that are already AI-native, pay for ChatGPT or Claude and a project-management tool, and are English-speaking.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/72) The near-term tester-recruitment ICP is intentionally narrower and explicitly working, not PamAI’s final market ICP.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/49) It focuses on English-speaking people carrying project execution in organizations around 1–20 people, close to decisions, without dedicated project-management capacity, and with a live project suitable for a 7–10-day test.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57)

## From discovery to evidence

Customer discovery qualifies problem context from sourced evidence and separates observed facts from inference; founder and operator titles are retrieval handles, not proof of fit.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/49) After a cold or warm call, the intake extracts a fixed six-field capture, logs the person or organization and interaction, and creates a follow-up when needed; the Network OS is the live record rather than a parallel spreadsheet.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/60) Questions ask about specific past behaviour instead of hypothetical usage, because hypotheticals produce false validation.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

Demand validation treats replies and signups as curiosity rather than willingness to pay. Real validation is a commitment of time, such as a pilot, or money, such as a deposit, LOI, or pre-payment.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59) The operating rule is to try one real thing, report one real result, and choose the next experiment rather than run several channels in parallel.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59) At the recorded 2026-08-23 baseline, cold LinkedIn outreach had sent 100 messages, received 15 replies, and produced zero committed testers.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59) The plan compares cold outreach, warm-network referrals, and one relevant community sequentially, then deepens the route that produces real commitment or revises the ICP and offer.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/59)

## Distribution and conversion

The network motion treats LinkedIn discovery as the top of funnel: acceptance unlocks research, sourced relevance informs a human outreach draft, and replies and testing become relationship outcomes.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57) Its V1 boundary is deliberately constrained to LinkedIn connections, with a maximum of 10 ordinary requests per day; Amir owns messages and personalized invitation notes, while email, Pam-Leads, CommentScout, and automated message sending are excluded.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/57) Since the [2026-07-18 cost and actor-contract audit](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/decision/215), paid network discovery and connection sending remain paused until typed actor adapters, atomic budget reservation, settled provider cost, one problem-context hypothesis, and the discovery scorecard are in place.

Outbound runs three motions. Broad, segmented outreach learns which personas, pains, triggers, and calls to action produce replies; high-value accounts receive the same direct structure with deeper account-specific research.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) A third, real-world invitation motion creates a lightweight public reason for strangers to self-select into conversation — a no-pitch cafe invitation that offers coffee, names builders broadly, removes sales pressure, and scripts the first line, with the learning unit a short conversation rather than a booked sales call.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) Replies and objections are treated as market feedback, not merely as a meeting tally.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) Channel choice can also be read from the receiving side: an accelerator-style application intake names the inbound source routes an organization already expects to attribute — accelerator or founder community, angel group, team member, in-person event, referral, social/online, and web search or AI recommendation — and the working rule is to match the outbound motion to the route rather than spray every channel.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/253) After discovery, prospect sales collateral turns the conversation into a tailored “Pam for X” document using the prospect’s own words and real product screenshots; it is separate from the investor-facing pitch deck.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/68)

## Emerging partner hypothesis

A first-pass R&D tax-credit channel map explores consultancy and referral ecosystems as a possible route to market, but it explicitly remains unvalidated web research and treats its claims as hypotheses rather than facts.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/75) Before treating the route as viable, the map recommends real conversations with startup-leaning firms to learn how their clients handle the problem and whether structured input from Pam would save partner time.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/75) It also warns that a generic offer to read GitHub and draft a UK R&D claim would face an existing direct software competitor, so the channel and differentiation need validation together.[^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/75)
