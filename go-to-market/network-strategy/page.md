# Network Strategy

PAM’s external-network strategy is a founder-led learning system: it seeks qualified relationships with people likely to test PAM, teach the team about project-execution problems, refer relevant users, or become advocates. V1 judges progress by conversations and product tests rather than impressions or raw connection count.^\[1\]^ (`data:57`) The network domain holds durable strategy and operating context for relationships, audiences, programs, research, and channel use; live operational network state is kept in Supabase rather than mirrored into Markdown.^\[2\]^ (`data:50`)

## Relationship doctrine

PAM’s operating thesis is that founder-led, relationship-led distribution is the fastest learning loop while the product is early. LinkedIn discovery creates the top of funnel; acceptance unlocks research, sourced relevance unlocks a human outreach draft, and replies and testing become relationship outcomes.^\[3\]^ (`data:57`)

## Audience and qualification

The defined recruitment audience is English-speaking people personally carrying project execution, prioritizing the UK, UAE, US, Canada, Australia, and Western Europe. Initial targets are organizations around 1–20 people where the potential tester is close to decisions, lacks dedicated project-management capacity, and has a live project suitable for a 7–10-day test.^\[4\]^ (`data:57`) Founder and operator titles are retrieval handles, not qualification.^\[5\]^ (`data:57`)

The 18 July 2026 discovery audit found that broad founder/owner and generic company-keyword searches produced local businesses, real estate, fitness, construction, recruiters, and other weak problem-fit rows; it concluded that a small company plus a founder title is not qualification.^\[6\]^ (`data:52`) Future discovery runs therefore require one problem-context hypothesis and the defined scorecard, rather than relying on title or company size alone.^\[7\]^ (`data:52`)

## LinkedIn channel policy

V1 uses LinkedIn connections only, with a maximum of 10 ordinary requests per day. Amir owns all messages and personalized invitation notes; email, Pam-Leads, CommentScout, and automated message sending are excluded.^\[8\]^ (`data:57`) LinkedIn handling must use Amir’s normal Chrome profile, device, and IP with visible sequential interaction. The operating policy caps ordinary no-note requests at 10 per Istanbul day, queues high-value profiles for Amir within five personalized notes per month, and allows Codex to inspect inboxes and replies but never send a LinkedIn message.^\[9\]^ (`data:55`)

Activity stops on CAPTCHA, verification, account warning, invitation-limit UI, unexpected login, Chrome failure, or other unusual friction. Acceptance rate, pending buildup, and warnings are tracked, and unauthorized automation remains a nonzero account risk.^\[10\]^ (`data:55`)

## Evidence-led channel learning

Channel selection is sequential rather than parallel: the live plan tries one real thing, reports one real result, and then chooses what to do next instead of running five channels at once.^\[11\]^ (`data:59`) Cold outreach, the warm network, and one relevant community are evaluated for real commitment, not just politeness; the route that produces that commitment is where the following phase goes deeper, while the others are dropped or revisited later.^\[12\]^ (`data:59`)

As of 23 August 2026, cold LinkedIn outreach had sent 100 messages and received 15 replies but produced zero committed testers.^\[13\]^ (`data:59`) The validation threshold is a commitment of time, such as a real pilot, or money, such as a deposit, letter of intent, or pre-payment, rather than a warm expression of interest.^\[14\]^ (`data:59`) Days 61–90 are reserved for deciding from real evidence whether the channel is repeatable or the ICP or offer needs to change.^\[15\]^ (`data:59`)

## Discovery controls and boundary

The 18 July 2026 audit also found that network discovery spent $1.246 while the Company Brain ledger reported only $0.16, and that two supposed zero-result Code Crafter searches had actually returned 100 and 60 rows but were misparsed under Harvest’s camelCase contract instead of Code Crafter’s snake_case contract.^\[16\]^ (`data:52`) Paid discovery and connection sending remain paused until typed actor adapters, atomic budget reservation, settled provider cost, one problem-context hypothesis, and the discovery scorecard are in place.^\[17\]^ (`data:52`)

The database is the canonical operational record for people, profiles, relationships, and activities; this strategy layer should not mirror a person’s profile or timeline into Markdown.^\[18\]^ (`data:50`) The resulting learning loop is complemented by [Customer Discovery](../customer-discovery/page.md), [Outbound Prospecting](../outbound-prospecting/page.md), and [Demand Validation](../demand-validation/page.md).
