# Negin

Within PamAI’s internal operating model, Negin leads product direction under the CEO and product-lead titles, covering the concepts behind Pam and the development of its features. In the small team, she also handles executive work directly: testing and outreach are personal responsibilities rather than delegated ones.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16)

## Product leadership

Her product method starts with a felt problem or ambiguity, asks what change should exist after the product is built, and insists on specificity about whom to design for first.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41) She brought that method to Pam’s founding reframe by moving the question away from copying conventional project-management features and toward changing how people do projects, including whether project work could be redesigned around AI from the center.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41)

Her philosophy adds a commitment to make things better, an ethical sense of right and wrong, and long-term value beyond shipping a feature.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41) When a product decision is off-vision, she diagnoses the problem, translates it into technical language, and persuades on the tradeoff rather than asserting taste. Amir puts in the building and coding effort, while they continually review the vision together.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41)

One concrete application is Foundation Chat: Negin designed Pam’s first conversation around consultant-style questions that create clarity before planning, using high-level questions instead of many small chatbot follow-ups.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41)

## Testing and outreach

Negin tests features herself each day, checking that they work, recording what breaks or feels off, and feeding the feedback to Amir for refinement and iteration.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16) She owns outreach by emailing and approaching people for Pam’s first testing group. Her personal LinkedIn presence is product-focused and professional, intended to build a network for recruiting testers.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16)

## Collaboration

Negin and Amir’s collaboration pattern predates Pam: when Amir was stuck or disappointed, he began a “clarify conversation” with her, and her role was to remind him of the bigger picture, the necessity of the work, the progress already made, and his vision. She then redesigned the product around that existing relationship and collaboration approach.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41)

She wants reasoning and options explained before action, including on small decisions, and asks that structural changes be confirmed before they are built out.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16)
