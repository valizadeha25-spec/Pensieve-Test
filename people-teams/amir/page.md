# Amir

Amir is described internally as PamAI’s CTO and co-founder, driving product, engineering, and technical direction. He is also the primary content voice on LinkedIn.^\[1\]^[^data:15]

His primary domains are content, technical architecture, and product direction.^\[2\]^[^data:15]

## Collaboration preferences

Amir prefers peer-level collaboration without hand-holding or over-explanation. He expects choices to be presented as tradeoffs rather than tutorials, pushes back on complexity, and prefers minimal, direct implementations. When working with him, the default is to read context, make the call, and explain the decision when it is non-obvious rather than narrate options that will not be pursued.^\[3\]^[^data:15]

## Content responsibilities

Amir writes content in his own words, often as a voice transcript. Brainstorming and research are support modes when needed; editing should remain light, limited to fixing typos, untangling sentences, and sharpening the close. The process must not produce a post from scratch, restructure it, replace his voice with cleaner prose, or invent facts, experiences, or opinions.^\[4\]^[^data:15]

On 2026-06-30, Amir and Niyayesh agreed that he would supply the journey/build-in-public raw material (the what, the why, the lesson) when asked, write the one-sentence main insight for each chosen content idea, and lead the storyline pass. He posts to his own personal Instagram account, while Niyayesh posts to the PAM professional account.^\[5\]^[^data:15]
