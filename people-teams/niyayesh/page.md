# Niyayesh

Within PamAI’s internal operating model, Niyayesh’s documented Marketing Director remit covers content creation and execution across LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube Shorts. It turns PAM’s strategy into posts, scripts, and a consistent production rhythm.^\[1\]^ (`data:19`)

## Content operation

Her working loop combines topic ideation and decision-making — deciding what to post, not only generating ideas — with video scripting, cross-platform adaptation, and execution.^\[2\]^ (`data:19`) The channel roles in her operating plan are:

- **LinkedIn:** editorial brand voice, 1–3 posts per week, rotating across The Problem, The POV, and The Proof pillars.^\[3\]^ (`data:19`)
- **Instagram:** reels-first, trend-aware, playful, and native to the platform, with 2–3 posts per week.^\[4\]^ (`data:19`)
- **Twitter:** PAM’s account, active from Phase 2 onward.^\[5\]^ (`data:19`)
- **YouTube Shorts:** reposts the same footage as Instagram Reels.^\[6\]^ (`data:19`)

## Goals

The plan progresses from brand recognition to engagement and then leads.^\[7\]^ (`data:19`) Its phases are:

- **Phase 1, June 8–July 15:** build presence and production rhythm, targeting 150–200 LinkedIn sign-ups while Instagram awareness starts to grow.^\[8\]^ (`data:19`)
- **Phase 2, July 16–August 15:** embed cross-platform execution fully and establish a consistent routine.^\[9\]^ (`data:19`)
- **Phase 3, August 16–September 8:** scale toward 500+ active paying users and 5,000+ Instagram followers.^\[10\]^ (`data:19`)

## Pipeline ownership

An agreement with Amir dated June 30, 2026 assigns Niyayesh responsibility for synthesizing the weekly content-idea list: research, journey material, and brainstorming produce 10 ideas, from which 5 are chosen. She owns format selection, visual planning, and scheduling for each piece, and posts to PAM’s professional Instagram account.^\[11\]^ (`data:19`) Once a piece has its main insight, she holds end-to-end execution ownership of the pipeline and resolves anything that does not move forward according to plan.^\[12\]^ (`data:19`)

## Working preferences

- She prefers simple explanations, with technical concepts taught gradually rather than assumed.^\[13\]^ (`data:19`)
- She wants the reasoning behind actions, especially structural ones, explained before the action is taken.^\[14\]^ (`data:19`)
- She values honest, direct feedback and wants ideas challenged when there is a good reason, rather than receiving agreement for its own sake.^\[15\]^ (`data:19`)
- For content strategy, she expects strategic marketing-specialist input grounded in facts about PAM and the market, not just execution.^\[16\]^ (`data:19`)
- When research is requested, she prefers thorough work that goes beyond the surface.^\[17\]^ (`data:19`)
