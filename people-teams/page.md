# People & Teams

Pam’s team is small and hands-on: executive work is handled directly, with outreach and testing done personally rather than delegated.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16) These are internal operating roles rather than offices of an incorporated company: PamAI is operated by Amir as an individual and is not yet incorporated.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/9) As of June 2026, Amir was described internally as CTO and co-founder driving product and engineering, Niyayesh as marketing director leading content strategy and distribution, and another co-founder as product lead and content executive.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/17)

## Standing ownership

- **[Amir](./amir/page.md):** CTO and co-founder, driving product, engineering, and technical direction, and the primary LinkedIn content voice.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15)
- **[Negin](./negin/page.md):** CEO and product lead for Pam’s concepts and feature development; she also owns outreach to recruit Pam’s first testing group.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16)
- **[Niyayesh](./niyayesh/page.md):** Marketing Director, owning content creation and execution across LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube Shorts and turning strategy into posts, scripts, and a consistent production rhythm.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/19)

## How work is coordinated

Product coordination runs through a feedback loop. Negin tests features, captures what breaks or feels off, and feeds that back to Amir for refinement and iteration.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16) Negin’s account of the founding relationship describes a recurring “clarify conversation”: whenever Amir felt stuck or disappointed, he began one with her, and she brought him back to the bigger picture, the necessity of the work, the progress already made, and his vision.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41) She describes this dynamic as predating Pam and informing the product redesign.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41)

In product disagreements, Negin says the case has to be earned rather than asserted as taste. Her contribution is to diagnose off-vision problems, translate them into technical language, and persuade on the tradeoff; Amir contributes the building and coding effort, and they continually review the vision together.[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41)

Content follows a complementary handoff. Amir supplies the journey/build-in-public raw material, writes the main insight for each chosen idea, and leads the storyline pass.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) Niyayesh synthesizes the weekly idea list, owns format selection, visual planning, and scheduling, and takes end-to-end execution ownership once a piece has its main insight.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/19)

Working style is adapted to the person carrying the responsibility. Amir prefers peer-level collaboration with trade-off-oriented choices and minimal, direct implementations.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/15) Negin wants reasoning and options explained before action.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/16) Niyayesh prefers simple explanations, candid strategic feedback, and thorough research.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/19)
