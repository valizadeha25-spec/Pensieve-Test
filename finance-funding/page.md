# Finance & Funding

PamAI is currently self-funded, with a raise in motion.^\[1\]^[^data:273] This page holds the standing financial picture — funding status, the traction carried in fundraising material, external funding activity, and open money questions; pricing and value capture live in [Business Model](../business-strategy/business-model/page.md).

## Funding status and the pre-seed deck

A pre-seed pitch deck exists as Pam-Pitch-Deck.pdf and is kept as background reference rather than folded into working project facts.^\[2\]^[^data:273] When the deck was read into Pam during the recorded design-intent audit walkthrough, Pam captured its team, market sizing, pricing, traction, and the ask, and reported that nothing in it contradicted the project's working facts.^\[3\]^[^data:273]

The traction figures extracted there: ~50 discovery calls run, with 85% converting into trial.^\[4\]^[^data:273] The demand-validation work behind those conversations is detailed in [Demand Validation](../go-to-market/demand-validation/page.md).

## Open money questions

Whether people who love the product will actually pay once credits run out is genuinely unresolved — left open deliberately in the working project facts.^\[5\]^[^data:273] The pricing and capture model this question feeds is tracked in [Business Model](../business-strategy/business-model/page.md).

## External funding activity

The FoundersEdge accelerator application form was captured and analysed on 2026-08-31 as application intelligence — showing what an accelerator-style gate asks before a conversation, not answers to draft.^\[6\]^[^data:272] The form requires founder and startup basics plus a pitch deck, optionally accepts a product video and deck-feedback flow, and its deeper survey evaluates founder-market fit, proof of building, customer contact, shipped progress, operating track record, founder tradeoffs, team structure, and equity.^\[7\]^[^data:272] ^\[8\]^[^data:272] Whether Negin intends to submit this application or treats it purely as a reference surface remains open.^\[9\]^[^data:272]
