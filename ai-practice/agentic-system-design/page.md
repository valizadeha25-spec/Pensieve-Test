# Agentic System Design

Agentic system design starts with the operator’s knowledge, lived workflow, and judgment rather than with a ready-made agent. The human remains the source of substance; AI amplifies, shapes, and scales it rather than originating it.[^\[1\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) Designing a system rather than copying one is intended to make it understandable enough to diagnose and repair while building the transferable skill of designing systems.[^\[2\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34)

The concept is still seed-stage and unbounded: it records that it is “seed, not developing” and that its two gates are empty.[^\[3\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34)

## Design loop

### 0. Establish a shared knowledge foundation

Create a durable context layer before a workflow. In the Company Brain build, a layered knowledge base sat underneath every workflow, carrying durable material such as identity, purpose, values, and audience rather than recreating that context for each task.[^\[4\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) The substrate can be a folder or knowledge base; the mechanism matters less than having something to build on.[^\[5\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34)

### 1. Observe the manual work

Keep doing the work by hand while watching it. Map the actual workflow, record its pain points, and learn which parts matter before designing automation.[^\[6\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) Start from a felt gap rather than a generic feature request: Negin’s design method treats the question as something that appears when a person feels something wrong, ambiguous, or missing, then moves through why and who.[^\[7\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41)

### 2. Study existing approaches as inputs

Only after the manual workflow is understood, study videos, GitHub, existing products, and other agentic systems. The method places this step after observation and treats what is found as inspiration, never as a copy.[^\[8\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) A reference-led approach makes that discipline concrete: collect examples for a known reason, identify the component or quality being borrowed, combine selected traits rather than copying one reference, and critique the result against the original direction.[^\[9\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/103)

### 3. Build a narrow version and idealize it continuously

Build with AI inside the foundation, then start narrow and iterate. The concrete articulation is: “start narrow, keep idealizing, keep iterating, don’t give up early.”[^\[10\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) Idealization is not a one-time perfect-state exercise; it is a continuous re-imagining toward the way the operator naturally thinks and wants to work.[^\[11\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) Add the next workflow only when it is needed, rather than designing an entire department in advance.[^\[12\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34)

### 4. Keep the judgment layer and close the loop

Decide explicitly what remains the operator’s. The method calls this the authenticity core: the operator remains the source.[^\[13\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) AI can handle aggregation and pattern-detection while people retain approval for consequential actions.[^\[14\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/296)

A workflow is not mature merely because it can call tools or run without supervision. Define the goal, verify the result against it, stop or retry within a bounded condition, and evaluate tool calls, intermediate behavior, and final output.[^\[15\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/296)

## Constraints that keep the system yours

### Synthesize; do not copy

Existing resources are ingredients, not substitutes for thinking. The method is to extract first-principles thinking, analyze the operator’s own problem and workflow, combine lessons, add original ideas, and design that synthesis.[^\[16\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) Copying a workflow without understanding it creates dependence: when output drifts or breaks, the user may not be able to tell that it is wrong or fix where the problem began, and the user does not build the skill of designing workflows.[^\[17\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/42)

### Earn complexity through friction

AI makes building easy, which creates a temptation to add capabilities before they are needed. Amir’s hard-won lesson is that every addition can actively create more failure.[^\[18\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/42) Company Brain’s build discipline was to add each road only after its friction had been felt by hand, because every system brings maintenance, cleanup, mental, and token costs.[^\[19\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35)

More instructions are not automatically more reliable. Over-constraining an agent can make it brittle; the recorded repair was to remove instructions and simplify the prompt and environment.[^\[20\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35) Pam’s founding account gives the same lesson: the fix was to simplify and give the model more room rather than add more structure and constraints.[^\[21\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/37)

### Own the substrate without assuming a bespoke application

Ownership is the enabler of adaptability: building rather than renting lets the system keep changing its tools, skills, automations, triggers, and workflows.[^\[22\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) Adaptation does not always mean building more software; the right substrate may be the least layered option that matches the operator’s tolerance for setup and need for portability.[^\[23\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/105)

## Architecture boundary

This method is not the same thing as a context-orchestration architecture. A key prior-art comparison is the Interpretable Context Methodology (ICM), a March 2026 paper by Van Clief and McDermott (arXiv 2603.16021) arguing that for sequential, human-reviewed, repeatable workflows a folder hierarchy plus markdown prompts plus local scripts is itself the architecture, formalised as a five-layer context hierarchy, five design principles, and Inputs/Process/Outputs stage contracts.[^\[24\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/241) The current concept describes the distinction as provisional: ICM is about how to structure context for an agent, while this method is about how a human decides what to build and where to keep judgment.[^\[25\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34)

ICM is intentionally bounded. Its synthesis excludes real-time multi-agent collaboration, high-concurrency systems, and complex automated branching; it also notes that it has not been tested through a controlled comparison and has been tested within a single model family.[^\[26\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/241)

A live crew is the neighbouring architecture rather than a replacement for ICM: one non-editing chief classifies and routes work to specialists with disjoint file ownership and receives summaries from an auditor and the editors, while ICM runs one agent through sequential stage contracts and the crew runs several isolated working contexts in parallel under a control plane.[^\[27\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/241) The crew becomes preferable only when work can be partitioned cleanly enough that parallelism is real and reconciliation is cheaper than sequential execution.[^\[28\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/241)

When a design does require several agents, separate responsibility before adding coordination. Sequential work with inspectable handoffs may use file-defined stages; concurrent specialists with live dependencies may need bounded messaging; either architecture still needs explicit jobs, permissions, verification, and recovery.[^\[29\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/297) A chief is a control plane, not a more powerful specialist: it surveys the bounded work surface, classifies observed problems, routes each to the specialist who owns that discipline, and recommends the final result while fixing nothing, with specialists editing disjoint files and an auditor reading across the system without editing what it evaluates.[^\[30\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/297) The crew's creator guidance also sets scaling thresholds: isolate colliding editors with worktrees, move beyond roughly five live agents into scripted workflow, and use managed infrastructure for unattended schedules and persistent state — with the durable lesson to externalize workflow state once coordination starts dominating the work.[^\[31\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/297) The roughly-five threshold is artifact guidance, not a measured performance boundary.[^\[32\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/270) Job titles and personas are not the mechanism — the working boundary is scope plus tools plus edit authority plus isolated context plus a return contract, with project-specific facts and rules in a shared house file and portable role behavior in agent definitions.[^\[33\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/297)

When a persistent agent carries ongoing responsibility for a domain rather than a single run, the recorded pattern is to design it as a control plane rather than a more capable generalist: it holds objectives and constraints, maintains the initiative and experiment portfolio, defines decision-ready briefs, routes execution to bounded specialists, reconciles cross-workstream findings, and updates strategy from evidence, while execution and audit remain independently owned and its state lives outside its own context window.[^\[34\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/270) State is layered rather than merged: project-specific truth and rules belong in the project house file, portable role behavior in the agent definition, the current initiative’s evidence and brief in per-run working context, and persistent initiative state in a durable substrate rather than hidden conversation memory.[^\[35\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/270) Each bounded initiative runs under an experiment contract defined before execution — hypothesis, owner, evidence, metric, stop condition, and next decision — an independent evaluator compares the result with that contract, and the decision and evidence are written back to durable state before the next loop opens.[^\[36\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/270) The pattern’s headline demonstration — a website-repair crew reported to surface 14 problems with four specialists completing in 27 minutes — is a demonstration claim, not an independently reproduced or Pam-validated outcome.[^\[37\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/270) A proposed application of this pattern to a persistent marketing strategist is described on [AI Marketing Workflows](../../content-brand/ai-marketing-workflows/page.md).

Memory is another separate architecture choice: retrieval-only memory and persistent state are different architectures, and retrofitting true state persistence is not trivial.[^\[38\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/142) See [Agent Memory](../agent-memory/page.md) for that layer.

Deciding what the agent reasons over comes before how it is orchestrated. A conversation Negin had with ChatGPT, shared verbatim into a Company Brain session on 2026-08-27, treats ontology-first design as that step: ontology asks what objects, states, relationships, rules, and actions exist in the agent's world, epistemology asks where the agent's information came from, how certain it is, and what it should trust, the ontological discussion happens before the choice of model, and the ontology supplies the world in which the agent's reasoning must operate.[^\[39\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/263) With agency in the picture, retrieval alone stops being enough: a raw LLM does not inherently know what launch, dependency, authority, approval, owner, or project state mean inside an organization, an agent needs an understanding of the world plus tools and rules to produce decisions and actions, and the implicit ontology humans carry is exactly what agents lack.[^\[40\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/263) The same conversation's sequencing principle is that the conceptual model is designed first and the storage architecture chosen second, on the argument that it is easy to build impressive graph infrastructure around a bad model.[^\[41\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/263) It also reads this as an industry direction: Forrester described data without explicit meaning as a foundational gap exposed by agentic AI in June 2026, and AWS announced AWS Context in June 2026 followed by a Context Ontology Accelerator on July 31, 2026 for machine-readable models of a company's customers, products, policies, relationships, and rules.[^\[42\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/263)

For PAM, the boundary stays open: as of 2026-08-27, no future ontology has been chosen, the current implementation combines a relational work hierarchy with a file/object-storage context layer, and whether that separation remains correct — and whether concepts such as Commitment, Decision, Event, Claim, or Risk become structured objects — is an open design question.[^\[43\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/185) The brainstorm folder behind this thread remains input, not doctrine.[^\[44\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/185) Its draft reality layer, candidate entity set, and stress test are tracked on [PAM Ontology Brainstorm](../../systems/product-architecture/ontology-exploration/page.md), and the verified current-state architecture on [Product Architecture](../../systems/product-architecture/page.md).

## Lived evidence

Company Brain provides a concrete example of the loop. It began as an empty directory; nothing was designed up front, and each piece was built when friction became real. As the system evolved, recurring thinking was held by the environment rather than redone manually.[^\[45\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/35) See [Company Brain](../../systems/company-brain/page.md).

Pam provides a second product-design example: its team mapped the current “doing a project” domain and workflow in detail before designing anything new on top of it.[^\[46\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/41) The product question then became how to design around AI rather than simply add AI to project management.[^\[47\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/65) These examples demonstrate the method without defining it by Pam’s particular product workflows.

## Current boundary

The concept's own record holds that until its boundary is defined, it is not a bounded framework.[^\[48\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) Its open questions are when copy-paste-and-move-on is genuinely the right call, when keeping judgment becomes a bottleneck, and whether manual-first observation makes sense for very small tasks.[^\[49\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34) For now, what holds is the method's center: keep the human as the source of the substance and let the AI amplify, shape, and scale it, never originate it.[^\[50\]^](https://app.pensieve.uk/r/506/data/34)
