# Overview

PamAI is an AI-native project-management product operated by Amirmohammad Valizadehmehrabani, an individual who is not yet incorporated.^\[1\]^ (`data:9`) Its agent helps users manage projects, conversations, files, schedules, integrations, and related work context.^\[2\]^ (`data:9`) PamAI positions itself as “Your AI Project Manager, not another project management tool.”^\[3\]^ (`data:14`) The product vision is an AI partner that understands the plan, watches the work, and takes the managing off the user’s plate.^\[4\]^ (`data:13`)

PamAI is currently offered to users outside the European Union and European Economic Area and is for users aged 16 or older.^\[5\]^ (`data:9`)

## How PamAI operates

PamAI stores and retrieves workspaces, projects, messages, files, and memory, and runs user-requested integrations and imports.^\[6\]^ (`data:9`) Its AI processing uses prompts, outputs, project context, files, and memory needed to answer user requests.^\[7\]^ (`data:9`) User content is sent to AI models through the Vercel AI Gateway.^\[8\]^ (`data:9`) AI requests use Zero Data Retention by default, and inference is restricted to a vetted allowlist of US/EU or Western/GDPR-aligned hosts. Provider training on interactive conversation data is an explicit profile choice and is off by default.^\[9\]^ (`data:9`)

PamAI collects data from users, invited workspace or project users, connected third-party services, payment and billing providers, and automatic service logs.^\[10\]^ (`data:9`) It uses that data to provide the product and agent, operate workspaces and integrations, process subscriptions and billing, secure and debug the service, prevent abuse, and meet legal, accounting, tax, and regulatory duties.^\[11\]^ (`data:9`)

## Product and operating boundaries

The documented integration surface includes Telegram, WhatsApp, Google Calendar, Notion, ClickUp, and coding-agent client data.^\[12\]^ (`data:9`) For Google-connected features, PamAI says it accesses only the Google user data needed for the requested feature and does not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or use it to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI/ML models.^\[13\]^ (`data:9`) PamAI does not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.^\[14\]^ (`data:9`)
